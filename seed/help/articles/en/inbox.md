The inbox gathers every conversation that matters to you: updates and replies on items you follow, and every time someone mentions you. Instead of opening item after item, you read everything in one stream, newest first, reply on the spot and mark what you've handled. The goal is simple: reach an empty inbox and know you haven't missed anything.

## What you'll learn

- How to open the inbox and what appears in it
- How to switch between all updates and mentions only
- How to reply straight from the inbox
- How to mark items as read and show what you've already read

## Opening the inbox

- **On a computer:** in the sidebar, click **Inbox**. The number next to the link is how many updates you haven't read yet.
- **On a phone:** in the bottom navigation bar, tap **Updates**. It also shows a count of what's unread.

The count updates in real time, without refreshing the page.

## What appears in the inbox

The inbox shows updates from the last six months, newest first:

- New updates and replies on items you follow
- Every update in which you were mentioned

Each entry shows who wrote it, what happened (**wrote on**, **replied on** or **mentioned you in**), the item name and the board, the time, the full text of the update, and the names of any attached files. Entries you haven't read yet have a different background.

Clicking the item name opens the item card, where you can see the whole thread, reply with formatting and files, and add a reaction.

![The inbox with unread entries and a reply box under a mention](images/inbox-1.webp)

> **Tip:** What reaches the inbox depends on following. If you want to see every update on a particular item here, click **Follow** on its card. If an item floods you, click **Following** to stop following it.

## Filter: all or mentions only

At the top of the inbox, choose:

- **All**: every update and mention.
- **Mentions only**: only updates in which you were mentioned. This is the quickest way to see what's waiting for your answer.

When there's nothing to show, you'll see the message **No new updates on items you follow**, and in mentions mode: **Nobody mentioned you lately**.

## Quick reply from the inbox

Entries in which you were mentioned, which usually ask something of you, show a short reply box under the update.

1. Type in the **Write a reply…** box.
2. Click **Reply** or press Enter.
3. The message **Reply sent** appears, and the reply is added to the update's thread on the item.

To reply with formatting, files or mentions of more people, open the item and reply from the item card.

## Marking as read

- To mark one entry, click **Mark as read** in its corner. It drops off the list and the count goes down.
- To clear the inbox in one go, click **Mark all as read** at the top of the screen.
- To see entries you've already read again, turn on the **Show read** switch.

When there are older entries, click **More** at the bottom of the list to load them.

> **Note:** Reading an update in the inbox counts as seeing it. The person who wrote the update will see you in its "Seen by" list.

## The inbox or the bell?

The two complement each other:

|  | The inbox | The bell (notifications) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What appears | The full text of updates and replies | A short message about each event |
| Event types | Updates on followed items and mentions | Also assignments, reminders, automations, shares, support tickets and more |
| Action | Read and reply in place | Clicking takes you to the relevant place |

## Frequently asked questions

**Why didn't an update on a certain item reach my inbox?**\
You probably don't follow the item. Open it and click **Follow**. From now on, new updates on it will reach you.

**I marked something as read by mistake. How do I get it back?**\
Turn on **Show read**. The entry appears in the list again and you can open the item.

**Does the inbox show updates I wrote myself?**\
No. The inbox shows only other people's updates.
