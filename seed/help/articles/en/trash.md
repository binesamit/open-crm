Deleted an item by mistake? No need to panic. Almost everything you delete — items, groups, boards, columns, views, updates, docs, dashboards, folders and even workspaces — goes to the Trash first and stays there for 30 days. During that time you can bring it back exactly where it was, with all its content.

## What you'll learn

- How to undo a delete right away, with one click
- How to find deleted things in the Trash
- How to restore an item whose parent was also deleted
- How to delete for good and empty the Trash

## Undo a delete right away

After every delete, a message at the bottom of the screen says the thing was moved to the Trash, with an **Undo** button. Clicking it brings back what you deleted immediately, without opening the Trash.

> **Tip:** Missed the message? You can also undo the last action in the board's activity log, or restore from the Trash as explained below.

## Open the Trash

1. Click your profile picture in the top bar.
2. Choose **Trash**.
3. The table shows, for each deleted thing: **Name**, **Type**, **Board** (where it was deleted from), **Deleted by**, **Deleted** (when), and **Deleted for good** — how many days are left, for example "in 12 days".

![The Trash with a list of deleted items and buttons to restore and delete permanently](images/trash-1.webp)

- Search in the **Search trash** box.
- Filter by type — **Item**, **Group**, **Board**, **Column**, **View**, **Update**, **Doc**, **Dashboard**, **Folder** or **Workspace** — or leave **All types**.

> **Note:** A regular user sees only what they deleted themselves in the Trash. Account admins see everything deleted in the account. Guests don't see the Trash.

## Restore

1. Find what you want to bring back.
2. Click **Restore**.
3. It goes back exactly where it was, and a message confirms it was restored.

### When the parent is in the Trash too

If you deleted an item and then also deleted its board or group, the item can't come back on its own — it has nowhere to go. In that case the **Its parent is in trash too** window appears, offering to restore the parent first. Click **Restore both**, and both come back together.

![The "Its parent is in trash too" window with the button to restore both](images/trash-2.webp)

## Delete for good

- **A single item:** Click the trash can icon in its row (**Delete permanently**) and confirm. After that it can't be restored.
- **Empty the Trash:** Account admins see the **Empty trash** button at the top of the page. It permanently deletes everything in the Trash — for all users in the account.

> **Note:** Anything that has been in the Trash for more than 30 days is deleted for good automatically. Account admins can set a rule in **Tidy up** that empties the Trash sooner.

## Trash or archive?

If something is no longer relevant but you might need it in the future, it's better to move it to the **Archive** instead of deleting it. In the archive it's kept with no time limit and isn't deleted after 30 days.

## Frequently asked questions

**I deleted an item two months ago. Can I get it back?**\
After 30 days the item is deleted for good and can't be restored. That's why the archive is the better choice for things you might need later.

**I can't find something someone else deleted in the Trash.**\
Each user sees only what they deleted. Ask the account admin — they see everything that was deleted.

**Does restoring also bring back the item's updates and files?**\
Yes. The item comes back with all its content.
