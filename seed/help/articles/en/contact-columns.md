On a board of clients, suppliers or leads, contact details are the most important information. The columns in this article keep each kind of contact detail in an orderly way and let you act on it with a click: send an email, dial, open a WhatsApp chat, send an SMS, open a website or see the address on the map. That's how a "contact list" becomes a real work tool.

## What you'll learn

- How to fill in email and link columns, including display text
- How the phone column's action menu works and how to set it up
- How to send an SMS from the board
- How to use the location, country and world clock columns

## Email column

1. Click the cell.
2. Type the address in **Email address**.
3. You can add **Display text**, for example "Dana – Accounting". If you leave it empty, the address itself is shown.
4. Click **Save**.

Clicking the address in the cell opens a new message in your email program.

## Link column

The **Link** column works the same way: type an **Address (URL)** and, if you like, **Display text** too. If you typed an address without https://, the system adds it for you. Clicking the link opens it in a new tab.

## Phone column

Type the number the way you usually do, with or without a country code. The system recognizes valid numbers based on the default country of the column or the account, so a number like 050-1234567 is recognized as an Israeli number.

### The action menu

Clicking a phone number opens an action menu:

- **WhatsApp** – open a chat with the number.
- **Telegram** – open a chat (by username, if a column is set up for it).
- **Call** – dial. On a computer with no dialing app, a window shows the number with an option to copy it and dial from your phone.
- **SMS** – send a message (see below).
- **Copy** – copy the number.

Ctrl+click (or a long press on a phone) runs the column's default action right away. Clicking the area around the number in the cell opens the number for editing. For a number that isn't valid, only call and copy are offered.

### Column settings

In the phone column's **Column settings** you can set:

- **Action menu when clicking a number** – when it's off, the number is a regular dial link.
- **Actions shown** – which actions appear in the menu and in what order (**Up** / **Down**).
- **Default action** – what happens on a long press or Ctrl+click.
- **Telegram username column** – a text column with the username (@name).
- **Default country** – for numbers typed without a country code, or **The account's setting**.

### Your personal preferences

Each user can decide for themselves what happens when they click a number. Go to **My profile**, and on the **Profile** tab scroll to the **Phone numbers** section. There, under **When I click a phone number**, you can choose whether to open a menu or act right away (for example **Straight to WhatsApp**), whether WhatsApp on a computer opens in the **App** or the **Browser**, and how to send SMS from a phone.

### Sending an SMS

1. In the number's action menu, choose **SMS**.
2. In the **Send SMS** window, choose a ready-made **Template**, or write **Free text** in the **Message** field.
3. The character counter shows how many characters you've written and how many messages that splits into.
4. Check the **Preview** and click **Send**.

The message is saved as an update on the item, so the whole team sees what was sent and when.

> **Note:** Sending SMS from the system requires setup by the account admins (a provider and a sender name), and they also decide who may send and what the quotas are. Guests can't send SMS. If sending isn't available, a message explains why.

## Location column

1. Click the cell and start typing an address (**Street, number, city…**).
2. Choose an address from the list of suggestions, or complete the address yourself and click **Save**.
3. The system locates the address on the map. While it's locating, **Locating…** is shown, and if the address isn't found you'll see the message **Address not found on the map**.

Located addresses appear as points in the board's **Map view**.

## Country column

Click the cell, type in **Search a country…** and choose from the list. The country is shown with its flag. The column is useful for filtering and grouping clients by country.

## World clock column

The **World clock** column suits teams and clients who work in different time zones.

1. Click the cell and search in **Search a city or timezone…**, for example "New York".
2. The cell shows the current local time, with a green dot during working hours and a gray one outside them.

In **Column settings** you set the **Time format**, **Working hours (in that zone)** and **Working days**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I pasted a list of phone numbers from Excel. Will they be recognized?**\
Yes. The system accepts numbers in any common format, including extension numbers and star (short) numbers, and recognizes the valid ones among them.

**Can I send an SMS to several items at once?**\
From the cell, a message goes to one number. For automatic sending, for example when a status changes, use an automation.

**Who sees my WhatsApp and dialing preferences?**\
Only you. Personal preferences don't change the column settings for anyone else.
