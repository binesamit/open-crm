Groups are the shelves of a board: every item belongs to one group, and each group appears as a separate section with its own name, color and header row. With groups you can divide the work by stage, month, team or any other split that suits you, and see at a glance how many items there are in each part.

## What you'll learn

- Add a new group and choose its name and color
- Change the name, color and order of groups
- Collapse and expand groups, and show summaries for each group
- Duplicate, move to another board, archive and delete a group

## Adding a new group

1. In the board header, next to the **Add item** button, click the small arrow (**Add a group**).
2. In the **New group** panel, type the **Group name**.
3. Under **Color**, choose a color. The system suggests a color that no group on the board has yet.
4. Click **Add**. The new group appears at the top of the board.

![Add group panel with a name field and color choice](images/groups-1.webp)

You can also add a group in an exact place: open the actions menu of an existing group (see below) and choose **New group above** or **New group below**.

> **Tip:** On a phone, adding a group is in the **More actions** menu (⋯) at the top of the board.

## The group actions menu

All group actions are gathered in one menu. To open it, hover over the group name and click the **⋯** that appears next to it, or right-click the group header. From the keyboard: focus the ⋯ button and press Shift+F10.

| Menu action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Collapse group** / **Expand group** | Hides or shows the group's items |
| **Collapse all groups** | Collapses all the groups on the board (also Ctrl+G) |
| **Rename** | Edit the group name in place |
| **Color** | Opens a color palette for the group |
| **New group above** / **New group below** | Adds a group in that position |
| **Duplicate group** | Creates a copy of the group with its items |
| **Move up** / **Move down** | Changes the group's position on the board |
| **Move to another board** | Moves the whole group to another board |
| **Archive** | Takes the group out of view and keeps it in the archive |
| **Delete group** | Moves the group to the Trash |

> **Note:** The menu shows only actions you're allowed to take. Someone with view-only permission mainly sees the collapse and expand actions.

![The group actions menu open next to the group name](images/groups-2.webp)

## Name and color

- **Rename:** Click the group name itself and type a new name, or choose **Rename** in the menu. Enter saves.
- **Color:** Choose **Color** in the menu and click the color you want. The palette stays open so you can try a few colors; clicking outside it or pressing Esc closes it. The color appears in the group name and in the bar along its edge.

## Collapsing, expanding and summaries

1. Click the arrow next to the group name to collapse or expand it.
2. To collapse all groups at once, choose **Collapse all groups** in any group's menu, or press Ctrl+G. When all groups are collapsed, **Expand all** appears in the same place in the menu.
3. A collapsed group is shown as a single frame with the number of items in it, and above each column you see its summary (for example the status distribution bar or a sum of numbers).

The system remembers which groups you collapsed, so on your next visit the board opens the way you left it.

**Group summaries:** At the bottom of each group there's a summary row. Click a cell in this row to choose the calculation for the column, for example **Sum**, **Average**, **Median**, **Minimum**, **Maximum** or **Count**. In **View settings** (the gear icon in the toolbar) you can turn the **Summary row in each group** on or off, and choose a **Summary next to the group name**: a column whose summary is shown right next to each group's name.

## Moving items between groups

- Drag an item by the drag handle at the start of the row and drop it in another group, in the exact place you want.
- Or open the item menu, choose **Move to group**, and then the group.
- To move several items together, select them and choose **Move to group** in the action bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.

## Duplicating, moving to another board, archiving and deleting

- **Duplicate group** creates a new group with the suffix "(copy)" and copies of all the items.
- **Move to another board** opens a window where you choose the destination board and map the columns, just like moving an item. The full explanation is in the article on moving items between boards.
- **Archive** takes the group off the board without deleting it. You can restore it from the archive page.
- **Delete group** asks for confirmation and moves the group to the Trash, where it's kept for 30 days. Right after deleting, a message appears with an **Undo** button.

> **Note:** You can't delete, archive or move to another board the only group on a board, because every board must have at least one group.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I drag a whole group to another place?**\
The order of groups is changed from the menu, with **Move up** and **Move down**. Dragging is available for items.

**I saw groups named "Today" or "Next week". Where did they come from?**\
The view is probably grouped by a column rather than by the board's groups. In the toolbar, click **Group by** and choose **Board groups** to go back to the regular groups.

**I deleted a group by mistake. What do I do?**\
Click **Undo** in the message that appears right after deleting, or press Ctrl+Z within a minute. After that you can restore it from the Trash or from the board's activity log.
