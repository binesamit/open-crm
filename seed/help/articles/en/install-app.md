You don't need to download an app from a store to work from your phone. The system installs straight from the browser to your home screen, with your account's icon and name, and opens like a regular app: full screen, no address bar, and with push notifications that arrive even when the app is closed. Installing takes less than a minute.

## What you'll learn

- How to install the app on iPhone and Android
- How to turn on push notifications on the device
- How to choose which notifications reach your phone and set quiet hours
- How to manage your devices
- What account admins can set

## Installing to the home screen

After you've opened the system on your phone a few times, a gentle bar appears at the bottom of the screen: **Add … to your home screen?** with the explanation **Opens like an app, without the address bar, with phone notifications.**

### On Android

1. When the bar appears, tap **Install**.
2. Confirm the browser's install window.
3. The icon appears on your home screen.

If the bar doesn't appear, you can also install from the browser menu (in Chrome: ⋮ → "Install app" or "Add to Home screen").

### On iPhone and iPad

On iPhone you install through Safari, and the bar shows three steps:

1. **Tap the Share button at the bottom**.
2. **Choose "Add to Home Screen"**.
3. **Tap "Add" — the icon appears on your home screen**.

![The "Add to home screen" bar on a phone with the three iPhone install steps](images/install-app-1.webp)

> **Tip:** Tapping **Not now** hides the bar for a month. You can install at any time without the bar, through the Share button in Safari or the browser menu on Android.

After installing, the home-screen icon carries the account's logo and name. When it opens, a screen in the account's color shows for a moment, followed by the screen the account admin chose, usually **My work**.

## Turning on push notifications

### The first time you open it from the icon

The first time you open the app from the home-screen icon, you'll be asked **Get notifications on this phone?**, with an explanation that only what matters will arrive, such as assignments and mentions.

1. Tap **Yes**.
2. Approve the permission request the phone shows.
3. The message **Phone notifications are on for this device** appears.

### From the settings page

If you skipped it, or if you work in a browser on a computer:

1. Open **My profile** → the **Notifications** tab (on a phone: **Me** → **Notification settings**).
2. In the **Phone** section, click **Turn on for this device**.
3. Approve the browser's permission request.

![The "Phone" section of the notification settings, with turning on for the device, the device list and quiet hours](images/install-app-2.webp)

> **Note:** On iPhone you can get push notifications only after the app is installed to the home screen and opened from the icon. If you see the message **On iPhone, install the app first to get notifications**, install it with the steps above and open it from the icon.

## What reaches your phone

The personal notifications table has a **Phone** column. By default, **I was assigned to an item** and **Someone mentioned me** are ticked in it, so your phone doesn't ring for everything. You can add or remove any type.

Tapping a notification on the phone opens the app exactly in the right place, for example in the item and the update where you were mentioned.

### Quiet hours

Under **Quiet hours**, choose a time range (**From** and **To**) when push notifications won't be sent, for example from 22:00 to 07:00. Notifications keep collecting in the bell as usual, and you'll see them when you open the app.

### Number on the icon

The **Number on the icon** switch shows the number of unread notifications on the app icon, just like the bell's counter. You can turn it off.

## Managing your devices

**My devices** lists all the devices where you turned on notifications, with the date each was added. The device you're using now is marked **This device**. To stop getting notifications on an old device, click **Remove** next to it.

## Version updates and working offline

- When a new version comes out, the bar **A new version is available** appears. Click **Refresh** to update, or **Later**. The system never refreshes while you're typing.
- When there's no internet connection, the **No internet connection** screen appears. When the connection comes back, everything reloads by itself. Changes aren't saved while you're offline, so you don't accidentally work on old data.

## Settings for account admins

- In **Administration** → **General** → **The phone app**, set the **Home-screen name**, the **Colour** and the screen it **Opens on**: **My work**, **Home** or **Updates**. The icon is the account's logo.
- In **Administration** → **Notifications**, set whether **Phone notifications in this account** are on, what the **Locked screen shows** (**Full content** or **Just "new notification"**), and whether to **Suggest adding to the home screen** and from which visit.

> **Note:** If the account admin turned phone notifications off, your settings show the message **The account admin turned phone notifications off.**

## Frequently asked questions

**Notifications are blocked. What do I do?**\
If you see the message **Notifications are blocked in the browser settings**, open the site settings in your browser (or the phone's notification settings), allow notifications and click **Turn on for this device** again.

**I installed it, but I'm not getting notifications.**\
Make sure the device is listed under **My devices**, that the **Phone** column is ticked for the types you want, and that you're not inside **Quiet hours**.

**Can I install it on a computer too?**\
Yes, in Chrome and Edge you can install from the address bar. Push notifications also work in the browser on a computer, without installing.
