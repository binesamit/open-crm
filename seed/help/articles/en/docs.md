Docs are the place for everything that doesn't fit in a table row: specs, procedures, meeting notes, quotes and team guides. Several people can write in the same doc at the same time and see each other, and a doc can include live boards and items, so the text and the work itself stay connected.

## What you'll learn

- How to create a doc and write in it with blocks
- How to embed boards and items and mention people
- How to comment on blocks and work with version history
- How to share a doc and set permissions
- How to export a doc to PDF, Markdown or HTML

## Creating a doc

1. In the sidebar, click **Add new** (**+**).
2. Choose **New doc**. The doc is created in the workspace and opens right away.
3. Click the title to give it a name, and press Enter.

You can also create a doc from the workspace page, in the **Docs** area.

> **Note:** Docs are available depending on the account's plan.

## Writing with blocks

Every paragraph in a doc is a block. Type **/** at the start of a line to open the **Add a block** list, keep typing to filter, and press Enter:

| Group | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Text and headings | **Text**, **Large heading**, **Medium heading**, **Small heading** |
| Lists | **Bulleted list**, **Numbered list**, **Checklist** |
| Structure | **Table**, **Quote**, **Code block**, **Divider**, **Callout**, **Toggle**, **Two columns** |
| Files | **Image**, **File** |
| Live from the platform | **Board or view (live)**, **Item card (live)** |

![An open doc with the “Add a block” list that opened after typing /](images/docs-1.webp)

The formatting bar at the top of the doc lets you change the **Block type**, make text bold, add a link, create lists, and use the **Insert** menu instead of typing /.

### Moving blocks and block actions

Hover over a block and a handle appears at the side (**Drag to move the block · click for actions**). Drag it to move the block, or click it for a menu: **Turn into**, **Duplicate**, **Copy link to block**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Comment on the block** and **Delete the block**.

### Mentions and embeds

- Type **@** to mention a teammate (they get a notification) or to link to an item, a board or another doc.
- The **Board or view (live)** block shows a board view inside the doc, with **Open board** a click away.
- The **Item card (live)** block shows an item with its current values.

> **Tip:** Anyone without permission for an embedded board or item sees **Restricted board** or **Restricted item** instead of the content: the doc doesn't reveal information they aren't allowed to see.

## Working together in real time

The people currently in the doc appear at the top, each in their own cursor color. Saving is automatic: the status switches between **Saving…** and **Saved**. Anyone with view-only permission sees the changes in real time but can't edit.

## Comments on blocks

1. Click inside the block you want to comment on.
2. Click **Comment on the block** (💬) in the bar.
3. Write the comment (you can mention people with @) and click **Send**, or press Ctrl+Enter.

The **Comments** button at the top of the doc opens the discussions panel, with **Open** and **Resolved** tabs. In each discussion you can reply (**Reply**), mark it with **Resolve**, or **Reopen** it.

## Version history

A version is saved automatically after editing. To work with versions:

1. Click **⋯** at the top of the doc and choose **Version history**.
2. Click **Save version** to save a named milestone (for example "Before client approval").
3. Pick a version from the list to view it, and click **Restore version** to bring it back.

> **Note:** Restoring replaces the content for everyone, but nothing is lost: the current content is saved as a version first.

## Sharing and permissions

Click **Share** at the top of the doc (available to the doc's owners and to admins). In the window, choose the **Doc type**:

| Type | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Main** | All workspace members see and edit the doc (account viewers can read only) |
| **Private** | Only people who were invited, at the level set for them |
| **Shareable** | Only people who were invited, and you can also invite guests from outside the company |

Under **Who is invited**, click **Add a person or team…** and set **Access** for each one: **Owner**, **Editor**, **Commenter** or **Viewer**. In the **Invite by email** area you can invite new addresses as a **Member** or a **Guest**.

![The doc sharing window with “Doc type”, the “Who is invited” list with permissions, and the “Invite by email” area](images/docs-2.webp)

> **Note:** Guests can only be invited to a **Shareable** doc. A guest sees the label **Shared with you** in the doc.

## More actions and the ⋯ menu

In the **⋯** menu at the top of the doc: **Add to favorites**, **Copy link**, **Version history**, **Export** (**PDF (print)**, **Markdown**, **HTML**), **Duplicate doc** and **Delete**.

## A doc inside an item

You can add a doc column to a board. Clicking **New doc** in a cell creates a doc for the item and opens it in a side panel; from there you can choose **Open as a page**. In the column settings you can pick a **Template for new docs**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I deleted text by mistake. How do I get it back?**\
Ctrl+Z undoes it right away. For older changes, use **Version history** and **Restore version**.

**Why can't I edit?**\
If a **View only** message appears, your permission is viewer (or commenter, who can only comment). Ask the doc's owner for editor permission.
