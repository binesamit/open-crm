Notifications make sure you know when you're needed: someone assigned you to a task, mentioned you in an update, replied to your update, or you forgot a running timer. All notifications arrive in the bell, and you can also get them by email and on your phone. The settings are personal and separate for each account, so everyone chooses exactly how much noise they're willing to get.

## What you'll learn

- How to use the notification bell
- How to choose a channel for each type of notification: bell, email or phone
- How to switch from an email per notification to a daily or weekly digest
- How to mute a whole board or reduce the notifications from it
- What account admins set as the default

## The bell

The bell is at the top of the sidebar on a computer, and in the top bar on a phone. A dot on the bell means there are unread notifications.

1. Click the bell. The **Notifications** window opens.
2. Notifications are grouped by **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Earlier**, and the names of people and items are highlighted.
3. Clicking a notification marks it as read and takes you to where it points: the item (on the updates tab, if it's a mention or a reply), a doc, a dashboard or a support ticket.
4. **Mark all as read** clears all notifications at once.
5. **Notification settings** at the bottom of the window takes you to your personal settings.

![The bell window with notifications grouped by today and yesterday](images/notifications-1.webp)

> **Tip:** Approval requests from AI agents arrive in the bell with **Approve** and **Reject** buttons, so you can decide without opening the run.

## Personal notification settings

1. Click your profile picture and choose **My profile**, then go to the **Notifications** tab. You can also get there from **Notification settings** in the bell window. On a phone: **Me** → **Notification settings**.
2. The **My notifications** table lists the notification types, and each type has three switches: **Bell**, **Email** and **Phone**.
3. Every change is saved immediately.

| Type | When it happens |
| --- | --- |
| **I was assigned to an item** | Someone added you to a people column |
| **Someone mentioned me** | Someone typed @ with your name in an update or a doc |
| **Updates and replies on items I follow** | A new update on an item you follow, a reply to your update, a reply in a doc discussion, and a doc or dashboard shared with you |
| **Reminders (forgotten timer)** | A timer that's been running for more than 12 hours, messages from automations, approval requests from agents, and system messages about the account |

![The My notifications table with switches for bell, email and phone](images/notifications-2.webp)

> **Note:** The **Phone** column works only after you've turned on notifications on the device. The full explanation is in the article "Installing the app on your phone and push notifications".

### How to get emails

In the **How to get emails** part, choose:

- **One email per notification**: an immediate email for every notification you've ticked for email.
- **Daily summary**: one email a day. Under **At**, choose the time it's sent.
- **Weekly summary**: one email a week. Choose **On** (the day) and **At** (the time).

## Notifications from a specific board

Sometimes one board sends too much. Instead of turning off a notification type across the whole account, you can deal with just that board.

1. In the board header, click **⋯** and choose **Notifications**.
2. Choose one of the options:

   - **Get notifications from this board**: the normal behavior.
   - **Custom — choose what comes from this board**: a window where you tick bell and email for each type, then click **Save**.
   - **Mute this board for me**: you won't get notifications from the board.
   - **Mute this board for everyone**: available to board owners and account admins only.
3. The current state is shown next to **Notifications** in the menu, for example **Muted** or **Custom**.

> **Note:** A custom board setting can only reduce. A type you turned off in your personal settings won't come from a board set to "Custom" either.

The boards you've muted appear on the personal settings page under **Muted boards**. Clicking **Unmute** brings them back.

## Account defaults (for admins)

Account admins decide what members get if they haven't changed the settings themselves.

1. Open **Administration** and go to the **Notifications** part.
2. Under **Account defaults**, set bell, email and phone for each type, and how emails are received.
3. A member's personal choice always overrides the default.

On the same screen admins also decide whether phone notifications are active in the account and what shows on a locked screen.

## Frequently asked questions

**I stopped getting emails altogether. Why?**\
Check that the **Email** column is ticked for the types you want, and that you didn't choose **Weekly summary** by mistake. Also check that the board isn't muted, and look in your spam folder.

**How do I stop notifications from just one item?**\
Open the item and click **Following** to stop following it. Direct mentions will still arrive.

**Are my settings shared across all my accounts?**\
No. Settings are saved separately for each account.
