Workspaces are how you split the account into large areas: a workspace for sales, one for operations, one for client projects. Inside each workspace you can arrange boards, docs and dashboards in folders and sub-folders, so even an account with hundreds of boards stays tidy and easy to navigate. Each workspace also has its own access setting: open to the whole account, or closed to workspace members only.

## What you'll learn

- How to create a workspace and switch between workspaces
- What's on the workspace page, and how to decide who sees it
- How to create folders and sub-folders and arrange boards in them
- How to move a board or folder to another workspace

## Creating a workspace and switching between workspaces

The sidebar shows one workspace at a time. Its name appears on the button under the **Workspace** heading.

1. Click the current workspace's name in the sidebar. The **Switch workspace** menu opens.
2. To go to another workspace, click its name in the list.
3. To create a new workspace, click **+ New workspace** at the top of the list, type the **Workspace name** and click **Create**.

![The workspace switcher menu in the sidebar, with the option to create a new workspace](images/workspaces-folders-1.webp)

> **Note:** Admins and team members can create workspaces. Viewers and guests can't.

## The workspace menu

Hover over the workspace name and click **⋯** (or right-click it). In the menu you'll find:

- **Workspace page** — the workspace's full page (details below).
- **Collapse all folders** / **Expand all folders** — closes or opens all folders and sub-folders at once.
- **New board**, **New doc**, **New dashboard**, **New folder**.
- **Rename**.
- **Delete workspace** — only possible when the workspace has no boards. Its folders are deleted along with it.

## The workspace page

The workspace page brings everything about the workspace together in one place:

1. **Name** — click the pencil icon next to the name (**Rename the workspace**) to edit it.
2. **Counters** — how many boards and folders the workspace has.
3. **New board here** — creates a board directly in the workspace.
4. **Description** — click **Add a description** (or **Edit the description**) and write what the workspace is about and who it's for. That way, people who join quickly understand where they are.
5. **Access** — a choice between **Open workspace** and **Closed workspace**, and the list of **Workspace members**.
6. **Boards** — all the boards, split by folder. Choose a **Layout** of **Cards** or **List**, and click a board's star to add it to your favorites.
7. **Docs** — the workspace's docs, with a **New doc** button.

![The workspace page with the description, the access setting and board cards by folder](images/workspaces-folders-2.webp)

You can also reach the workspace page from the home screen, by clicking the workspace name under **Workspaces**.

## Open and closed workspaces

|  | Open workspace | Closed workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who sees the workspace | All account members | Only workspace members and account admins |
| Main boards in the workspace | Visible to all account members | Visible to workspace members only |
| Private and shareable boards | According to each board's subscribers | According to each board's subscribers |

To manage workspace members, in the **Workspace members** section of the workspace page:

1. Click **Add member** and choose a person from the account. They're added right away.
2. To add someone who isn't in the account yet, use **Invite by email**. New people get an invitation to the account and join the workspace when they accept it.
3. To remove a member, click the X next to their name.

> **Note:** Guests aren't added to workspaces. They only see shareable boards they were invited to.

## Folders and sub-folders

1. In the sidebar, click the **+** next to the **Workspace** heading and choose **New folder**.
2. Type the **Folder name** and click **Create**.
3. To create a folder inside a folder, open the folder's **⋯** menu and choose **New sub-folder**. You can nest up to three levels.

Each folder's **⋯** menu also has **New board here**, **New doc here**, **New dashboard here**, **Rename** and **Delete**.

> **Note:** Deleting a folder also deletes its sub-folders, but not the boards: they move to the root of the workspace.

### Arranging by dragging

Drag a board, doc or dashboard onto a folder to put it in, or between rows to change the order. To take something out of a folder, drag it to the **Drop here to take it out of a folder** area. You can also choose **Move to…** in the board's **⋯** menu and pick a folder (or **No folder**).

## Moving to another workspace

1. Open the **⋯** menu of the board, doc, dashboard or folder in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Move to workspace…** and pick the target workspace.

When you move from a closed workspace to an open one, the app asks you to confirm: a main board becomes visible to everyone in the account, while private and shareable boards keep their subscribers and permissions.

## FAQ

**Why can't I delete a workspace?**\
You can only delete a workspace that has no boards. Move the boards to another workspace, or delete or archive them, and then try again.

**Who can change a workspace's access?**\
Anyone allowed to edit the workspace (workspace owners and account admins). Whoever closes a workspace is automatically added to it as a member.

**Do folders affect permissions?**\
No. Folders are only an organizing tool. Permissions are set at the workspace and board level.
