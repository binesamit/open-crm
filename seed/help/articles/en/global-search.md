Can't remember which board the client is on? Looking for an update someone wrote last week? Global search scans the whole account at once: items, boards, docs, dashboards, updates, files, people and tags. Results appear as you type, sorted by type, and you can move between them using only the keyboard.

## What you'll learn

- Open search from anywhere in the platform
- Read the results and open them from the keyboard
- Search by tag and include archived items
- Use commands to move quickly between pages

## Open search

There are a few ways, and they all open the same window:

- Press **Ctrl+B** (or ⌘+B on a Mac) from any screen.
- Click the search field at the top of the sidebar (**Search boards, items and people**).
- When the sidebar is collapsed, click the magnifying glass icon.
- On a phone, tap the magnifying glass icon in the top bar.

## Search and results

1. In the **Search items, boards, updates and people** field, type at least two letters.
2. Results appear as you type, divided by type: **Items**, **Boards**, **Docs**, **Dashboards**, **Updates**, **Files**, **People** and **Tags**. The text you searched for is highlighted in each result.
3. Next to each result is a detail that helps you identify it: an item's board and group (and when the match was in a cell rather than the name, the text that was found too), a board's workspace, who wrote an update and on which item, or a person's email.
4. Move between results with the up and down arrows, and press **Enter** to open one. You can also click a result with the mouse. **Esc** closes the window.

![The global search window with results divided into items and boards](images/global-search-1.webp)

An item opens directly in its item card, inside its board. A board, doc or dashboard opens on its own page, and an update opens in the item card where it was written.

> **Note:** Search shows only what you're allowed to see. Items from private boards you're not a member of, or items with restricted access, won't appear in the results.

## What search finds

- **Items** – by name, by text in their cells (for example a phone number, email or note), and also by the name of a tag attached to them.
- **Boards and dashboards** – by name.
- **Docs** – by name and also by the text inside them.
- **Updates** – by what was written in them.
- **Files** – by file name, in Files columns and in files attached to updates.
- **People** – by name and email. Guests don't see people results.
- **Tags** – by tag name.

## Search by tag

1. Type **\#** followed by the tag name, for example `#urgent`. In this case a single letter is enough.
2. The matching tags appear at the top of the results, and below them **Items tagged \#…**.
3. Clicking a tag in the results narrows the search to items with exactly that tag.

## Search the archive

By default, archived items don't appear in the results. Check **Include archive** in the search window to include them too. Results from the archive are marked **Archived**.

## Recent searches

When you open search without typing, the **Recent searches** list appears. Clicking one runs it again. The list is saved to your account and appears on every device. **Clear recent searches** deletes it.

## Commands: jump quickly between pages

Type **\>** at the start of the field, and **Commands** appear for going straight to pages:

| Command | Where it takes you |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The account's home page |
| **My work** | Everything assigned to you |
| **Inbox** | Your updates and mentions |
| **Notification settings** | Notification preferences in your profile |
| **API tokens** | Token management in your profile |
| **Automations** | The automations center |
| **Archive** | The archive page |
| **Trash** | Deleted items |

Keep typing after the **\>** to narrow the list, and press Enter.

![The list of commands in the search window after typing >](images/global-search-2.webp)

## Global search vs. board search

|  | Global search | Board search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortcut | Ctrl+B | Ctrl+F |
| Where it searches | The whole account | The current board only |
| What it shows | A list of results to open | Filters the board itself, including subitems |

## Frequently asked questions

**I searched for an item I'm sure exists, and nothing was found. Why?**\
Check that you typed at least two letters, that the item isn't archived (or check **Include archive**), and that you have access to its board. Items deleted to the Trash don't appear in search.

**Does search find values from formula columns too?**\
Search checks the text stored in cells. Values calculated when displayed, such as formula and mirror, aren't included.

**Can I find a doc by a word written inside it?**\
Yes. Search also checks the text of docs, and the result shows a snippet of the text around the word you searched for.
