The archive is the place for things you're done with but don't want to delete: a project that closed, a client you've finished working with, a group of tasks from last quarter. Whatever you archive disappears from the board, the lists and regular search, so your workspace stays clean, but it's kept with no time limit and you can bring it back at any moment.

## What you'll learn

- The difference between the archive and the Trash
- How to archive an item, a group or a board
- How to find and restore something from the archive
- How to search inside the archive too

## Archive or delete?

|  | Archive | Trash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When to use it | The work is done, but the information still matters | Something unneeded or created by mistake |
| How long it's kept | With no time limit | 30 days, then deleted permanently |
| What you can move there | Items, groups and boards | Almost everything: items, groups, boards, columns, views, updates, docs and more |

## Archiving

**An item:**

1. Hover over the item row and click its actions menu.
2. Choose **Archive**. The item disappears from the board and a message says it was archived.

**Several items at once:** Check them in the checkboxes, and in the actions bar at the bottom click **Archive**.

**A group:** In the group's menu (next to the group name), choose **Archive**.

**A board:**

1. In the sidebar, hover over the board name and click its actions menu.
2. Choose **Archive**. The board disappears from the sidebar.

![An item's actions menu with the Archive option](images/archive-1.webp)

> **Tip:** You can archive automatically. Automations have an **archive the item** action, for example "When the status changes to 'Done', archive the item". Account admins can also turn on a nightly cleanup rule that archives items completed long ago.

## The archive page

1. Click your profile picture in the top bar.
2. Choose **Archive**.
3. You'll see all the items, groups and boards that were archived, on boards you have access to, with the name, the type, who archived it and when.
4. Search in the **Search the archive** box, or filter by type: **Item**, **Group** or **Board**.

![The archive page with a list of items and boards and Restore and Open buttons](images/archive-2.webp)

## Restoring from the archive

- Click **Restore** next to what you want to bring back. It returns exactly where it was, and a message says it was restored from the archive.
- Click **Open** to take a look without restoring.

**An archived board** can be opened and read. A banner appears at its top: **This board is archived — it is left out of lists and search.** Click **Restore** in the banner to bring it back to the sidebar.

## Search that includes the archive

In the system's global search, you can check **Include archive** to also find things that were archived. Such results are marked **Archived**.

> **Note:** Guests don't see the archive page. The page shows only what's on boards you have access to.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see the option to archive. Why?**\
Archiving requires delete permission on that board. For groups, you can't archive the last group left on the board.

**I archived a board. What happened to its items?**\
The whole board, including all its items, is kept as is. Restoring brings all of it back.

**How long are things kept in the archive?**\
With no time limit. They stay there until you restore or delete them.
