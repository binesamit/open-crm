Access to Bines OS is by invitation only: the account admin (or someone allowed to) sends you an invitation, and from then on you can sign in several ways: with a password, with a one-time link sent to your email, or with a Google account. This article covers all the ways, what to do when you've forgotten your password, and switching between accounts when you're a member of more than one organization.

## What you'll learn

- How to join for the first time from an invitation
- The three ways to sign in
- How to reset your password, and what to do when the email doesn't arrive
- How to choose an account and switch between accounts
- How to sign out

## First sign-in: joining from an invitation

When you're invited, you get an email with a personal link (or the person who invited you sends you the link over WhatsApp or SMS). The link opens a screen headed **You're invited to** and the account name, with the address the invitation was sent to and the role you were given below it.

1. If you don't have a user in the system yet, fill in your **Full name** and a **Password** (at least 10 characters). The email address is already filled in and can't be changed.
2. Click **Choose a password and join**. You go straight into the account that invited you.
3. If you already have a user (for example in another account), click **I already have a user — sign in**, sign in as usual, and then click **Join the account**.

> **Note:** The invitation is personal and meant for a specific address. If you're signed in with a different address, a message says the invitation was sent to another address; sign out and sign in with the right one. If you see the message **This invitation is invalid, revoked or expired.**, ask the person who invited you to send a new link.

## Ways to sign in

The **Sign in to Bines OS** screen has three options:

1. **Email and password**: type your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**.
2. **Email link**: type only your email address and click **Email me a sign-in link**. Check your inbox and click the link that arrived. This is the easy way for anyone who hasn't set a password.
3. **Google**: click **Continue with Google** and connect with a Google account whose address matches the invited address. The button appears only when the option is turned on in the system.

![The sign-in screen with the email and password fields, the email link button and Continue with Google](images/sign-in-1.webp)

If you've turned on two-step verification, after any of these you'll also be asked to type a 6-digit code from the authenticator app on your phone.

> **Tip:** After several failed attempts in a row, you'll see the message **Too many attempts. Try again in a few minutes.** This protects your account. Wait a little and try again.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password**.
2. Type your email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email (check the spam folder too). The link is valid for one hour.
4. On the **Choose a new password** screen, type a new password and confirm it, then click **Save and sign in**.

**Email not arriving?** You can sign in with Google (if available), or contact your account admin: admins can generate a **One-time sign-in link** for you from the Users screen in Administration and send it to you any way they like.

## Choosing an account

If you're a member of only one account, the system takes you straight into it. If you have several accounts, the system remembers the last account you worked in and opens it. Otherwise, the **Choose an account** screen appears, asking **Which account do you want to open?**, with all your accounts and your role in each.

If there are invitations waiting that you haven't accepted yet, they appear on the same screen under **Pending invitations**, with a **Join** button next to each.

## Switching between accounts

1. Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Switch account**. The option appears only when you're a member of more than one account.
3. The submenu lists all your accounts, with the logo and your role in each; the current account is marked with ✓. Click the account you want to switch to.

![The avatar menu with the Switch account submenu](images/sign-in-2.webp)

On a phone: go to the **Me** tab in the bottom bar, where you'll find the list of accounts under **Switch account**.

## Signing out

Open the avatar menu and choose **Sign out** (on a phone, at the bottom of the **Me** tab). It's always a good idea to sign out when working on a shared computer.

## Frequently asked questions

**I tried to send a sign-in link and got nothing. Why?**\
For security reasons, the system doesn't reveal whether an address is registered. If the address isn't a member of any account, no link is sent. Make sure you're using the address the invitation was sent to.

**An account shows up for me with the label "Suspended". What does that mean?**\
The account has been suspended and isn't available for work right now. Contact the account admin in your organization.

**Can I sign in with a password if until now I've only signed in with a link?**\
Yes. In your profile, on the **Sign-in & security** tab, you can create a password at any time.
