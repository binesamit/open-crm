Your profile is how colleagues recognize you in the system: in mentions, people columns, updates and notifications. Your profile also holds your personal preferences — the interface language, time zone, how the system is displayed and more. All the settings here are personal: they affect only you, not the other users in the account.

## What you'll learn

- How to get to the profile page and what's in each tab
- How to upload a profile photo and position it
- The difference between personal details and account details
- How to change the appearance, color theme, text size and density

## Opening the profile page

1. Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **My profile**.

On a phone: go to the **Me** tab in the bottom bar.

The page has several tabs: **Profile**, **Sign-in & security**, **Notifications**, **API tokens** and **Appearance**. The **API tokens** tab appears only for admins and members — not for viewers or guests.

## Profile photo

1. At the top of the profile page, click the photo (or the letter shown in its place).
2. Choose **Upload profile photo** and pick a PNG, JPEG or WebP file up to 5MB. You can also simply drag an image file onto the current photo.
3. In the window that opens, drag the image to position it, and zoom in or out with the **Zoom** slider, the mouse wheel or a pinch on a touch screen. Confirm to save.

To change the position of an existing photo, choose **Adjust position and zoom**, and to remove it — **Remove photo**. The same options are also available from the avatar menu, under **Profile photo**.

## Personal details and account details

The **Profile** tab has two parts, and it's important to understand the difference between them:

| Part | Fields | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal details** | **Full name**, **Birthday**, **Language**, **Time zone** | The same in all your accounts |
| **Details in this account** | **Job title**, **Department / team**, **Phone** | Only in the current account |

So, for example, you can be "Marketing manager" in one account and "External consultant" in another.

1. Update the fields you want.
2. Type the **Birthday** in DD/MM/YYYY format, or pick it from the calendar with the button next to the field.
3. Under **Language**, choose **עברית** or **English**.
4. Under **Time zone** you can keep the **Account default** option, or choose a different time zone — useful when you're working from another country.
5. Click **Save**. The message **Profile saved** appears.

### Phone numbers

In the same tab, in the **Phone numbers** part, you decide what happens when you click a phone number in the system:

- **When I click a phone number** — open an actions menu, use the column's default, or go straight to WhatsApp, Telegram, a call, SMS or copying the number.
- **WhatsApp on a computer** — **Automatic**, **App** or **Browser**.
- **SMS on a phone** — **Ask each time**, **From the system** (saved as an update on the item) or **From the device**.

## Appearance

The **Appearance** tab brings together all the visual preferences. Every change applies immediately, so you can try things out and compare.

1. **Appearance** — **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (following the device setting).
2. **Text size and density**:

   - **Text size** — **Small**, **Normal**, **Large** or **Extra large**.
   - **Table density** — **Compact**, **Normal** or **Comfortable**. Especially useful if you work with large boards.
   - **Animations** — **Device setting**, **Full**, **Reduced** or **Off**.
   - **Only on this device** — when the switch is off, your choice is saved in your profile and reaches all your devices. When it's on, the current device keeps its own setting (for example, larger text on your phone).
3. **Colour theme** — **Deep green (default)**, **Ocean**, **Plum**, **Graphite** or **Terracotta**. Each theme has a light and a dark version.
4. **Sidebar** — the sidebar's shade: **Dark** or **Light**.

![The appearance tab with mode, text size, density and color theme choices](images/profile-settings-2.webp)

> **Tip:** You can also quickly switch light/dark mode, color theme and sidebar shade from the avatar menu, under **Appearance** — without opening the profile page.

> **Note:** In the dark interface the sidebar is always dark, even if you chose a light shade.

## Frequently asked questions

**I changed my name. Will colleagues see the change?**\
Yes. The updated name appears everywhere — in people columns, updates and mentions — and in all your accounts.

**Where do I choose which notifications to get?**\
In the **Notifications** tab of the profile page. The full settings are explained in the article on notifications.

**How do I change my password?**\
In the **Sign-in & security** tab. See the article on password and two-step verification.
