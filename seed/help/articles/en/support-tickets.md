When you run into a problem or a question the help center didn't solve, the support team is here for you. You open a ticket right from inside the system, and it already includes the technical information that helps us understand what happened, such as the page you were on and your browser. After that, an orderly conversation takes place inside the ticket, and you get a notification and an email for every reply and every status change.

## What you'll learn

- How to open a ticket and what to write in it
- What the system attaches automatically, and how to remove details before sending
- How to follow tickets, reply, close and rate them
- How admins approve temporary support access to the account
- Who can open tickets, and how admins decide that

## Opening a ticket

1. Click **?** (**Help**) and choose **Open a ticket**. You can also open a ticket from the bottom of the help center panel, or from the **My tickets** screen.
2. The **New support ticket** window opens. Fill in the **Subject**: a short sentence describing the problem.
3. As you type the subject, matching articles appear below it under **These articles may help**. It's worth a look, because sometimes the answer is already there. If an article solved the problem, click **Yes** under **Did this help?** and the window closes without opening a ticket.
4. In the **Description**, explain what you tried to do, what actually happened and what you expected to happen.
5. Choose a **Category** and **Priority** (**Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**), if they're shown.
6. Attach screenshots or files with **Attach a file**.
7. Click **Send**. The message **Ticket … opened** appears with the ticket number.

![New support ticket window with article suggestions below the subject](images/support-tickets-1.webp)

> **Tip:** A screenshot of the problem saves a lot of back-and-forth. The more precise the description, the faster the solution arrives.

### What's attached automatically

The **Attached automatically** part shows details the system collects to help us understand what happened:

- **Page**: the screen you were on when you opened the ticket.
- **Browser and system**: the browser type and operating system.
- **Error**: recent errors that appeared on the screen, if there were any.

You can remove any of these before sending by clicking **Remove**, and bring it back by clicking **Keep**.

## Following your tickets

1. Click **?** and choose **My tickets**.
2. On the **My tickets** screen, switch between **Open** and **Closed**.
3. Each ticket shows its number, subject and status. Click a ticket to open it.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | The ticket was received and is waiting to be handled |
| **Open** | The support team is working on it |
| **Waiting for you** | Support is waiting for a reply or information from you |
| **On hold** | Handling is temporarily paused |
| **Solved** | Support marked the problem as solved |
| **Closed** | The ticket is finished |

When support replies or changes the status, you get a notification in the bell and an email too. Clicking the notification opens the ticket.

## Conversation inside the ticket

The ticket page shows the whole conversation, and the team's replies are marked **Support**.

1. Write in the **Write a reply…** box and click **Send**.
2. When the problem is solved, click **Close the ticket**. If support has already marked the ticket as solved, the button is called **It is solved, close it**.
3. If the problem comes back, click **Reopen** on a closed ticket. Replying to a ticket marked as solved also reopens it.

![Ticket page with the conversation, the reply box and the close button](images/support-tickets-2.webp)

### Rating support

When the ticket is solved, the question **How was the support?** appears. Choose a rating from 1 to 5, add a comment if you like, and click **Send rating**.

## Account access request

Sometimes the support team needs to look at your boards to understand the problem. In that case the team sends an access request from within the ticket, and it appears at the top of the ticket page under **Access to the account**, with the type of access (**View only** or **View and edit**) and the duration.

- Account admins get a notification and an email, and decide by clicking **Approve** or **Reject**.
- Members who aren't admins see the message **The account's admins decide on the request.**

> **Note:** Approval creates temporary support access only. It's canceled automatically when the ticket is closed, and admins can revoke it at any time in **Administration** → **Security**. Everything support sees and does is logged.

## Who can open tickets (for admins)

In **Administration** → **Security**, in the **Support tickets** part, you decide:

- **Who opens tickets**: **Every member** or **Admins only**.
- **Members see**: **Their own tickets** or **All of the account's tickets**.

Admins always see all of the account's tickets. If only admins may open tickets, members see the message **In this account only admins open support tickets. Ask an account admin.**

## Frequently asked questions

**The "Open a ticket" option is grayed out for me.**\
The account may allow only admins to open tickets, or opening tickets may be temporarily turned off. Contact the account admin.

**I got a reply by email. Can I reply directly from the email?**\
The email has an **Open the ticket** link. Click it and reply from the ticket page, so the reply is saved in the conversation.

**Can support get into the account without approval?**\
No. Access to the account's data opens only after an account admin approves a request, and it's limited in time.
