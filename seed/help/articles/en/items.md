An item is a row on a board: a client, a task, a deal or anything else you track. Every item has a name and a cell in each of the board's columns, and behind every row there's a full item card with updates, files and history. In this article you'll find all the ways to create items and work with them day to day.

## What you'll learn

- Add items in several ways
- Rename items, fill in values and open the item card
- Duplicate an item, with or without its updates
- Reorder items by dragging and move them between groups
- Archive or delete an item

> **Note:** On some boards items go by a different name, such as "Client" or "Task". In that case the buttons are named to match, for example **New client**.

## Adding an item

**From the header:** In the board header, click **New item**. A new item is created at the top of the first group; then click its name to give it a name of your own.

**Inside a group:** At the bottom of every group there's an empty row that says **+ Add item**.

1. Click the row and type the item's name.
2. Press Enter. The item is added at the end of the group, and the field stays open so you can add the next item right away.

![The add item row at the bottom of a group, with an item name typed in](images/items-1.webp)

**Below an existing item:** Open the item menu and choose **Add item below**. An item called **New item** appears right after the item you chose.

> **Tip:** Have lots of rows to add or update at once, for example from Excel? Use **Import and edit**, where you can paste a whole table and check everything before saving.

## The item menu

Almost every action on an item is in one menu, which you can open in three ways:

- Hover over the row and click the **⋯** at its start
- Right-click the row
- Focus the row and press Shift+F10

The menu includes, among others: **Open**, **Open in new tab**, **Copy link**, **Copy ID**, **Follow item**, **Rename**, **Add item below**, **Add subitem**, **Duplicate**, **Duplicate with updates**, **Move to group**, **Move to another board**, **Make a subitem of…**, **Archive** and **Delete**. If the board has an item key set up (for example `BT-0042`), **Copy key** appears too. The menu shows only the actions you're allowed to do.

![An item's action menu, opened from the ⋯ button on the row](images/items-2.webp)

## Name, values and the item card

- **Rename:** Click the item name, type and press Enter. You can also press F2 or choose **Rename** from the menu. Esc cancels.
- **Open the item card:** Click the empty part of the name cell (beside or after the name), or choose **Open** in the menu. The card opens over the board, with the item's fields, updates and activity.
- **Fill in values:** Click a cell to edit it. Each column type opens in an editor that suits it: a list of labels for status, a calendar for a date, a list of people for a people column, and so on.
- **Keyboard navigation:** Arrow keys move between cells, Tab goes to the next cell, Enter opens the cell for editing, Delete clears it. You can select a range of cells with Shift and the arrow keys, and copy, cut and paste with Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V, including between the board and Excel.

> **Tip:** Right-clicking a cell opens the cell menu: **Edit**, **Copy**, **Cut**, **Paste**, **Clear cell** and **Open item**.

### A name built automatically

The board owner can set each item's name to be built from other columns, for example "Client – Date". The setting is in the name column's menu, under **Item name source**, where you choose between **Free text**, **From a column** and **Template**. On such a board you can't change the name directly: change the values in the columns it's built from, and the name updates by itself.

## Duplicating an item

1. Open the item menu.
2. Choose **Duplicate** to copy the item with all its values, or **Duplicate with updates** to copy its update threads too.
3. The copy appears right below the original, with "(copy)" added to its name.

The message that appears after duplicating has an **Undo** button, in case you change your mind. To duplicate several items together, select them and choose **Duplicate** in the actions bar.

## Reordering and moving between groups

- **Dragging:** Hover over the row and grab the drag handle at its start. You can also long-press the item name and drag. Drop the row where you want it, in the same group or in another group.
- **From the menu:** Choose **Move to group** and then the target group.

> **Note:** When the view is sorted by a column, the order follows the sort. The manual order comes back when you remove the sort.

## Archive and delete

- **Archive** takes the item off the board but keeps it with no time limit on the archive page. You can bring it back from there at any time.
- **Delete** (also the Delete key on selected items) asks for confirmation and moves the item to the Trash. You can restore it from there within 30 days.

In both cases a message appears with an **Undo** button, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last action within a minute.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between the archive and the Trash?**\
The archive is for items you're done with and want to keep, and it keeps them with no time limit. The Trash is for things you deleted, and items in it are deleted permanently after 30 days.

**Why can't I rename an item?**\
You may have view-only permission, or the board's names may be built automatically from other columns. In the second case, hovering over the name shows which columns it's built from.

**How do I send a teammate a direct link to an item?**\
In the item menu, choose **Copy link** and paste the link anywhere. Whoever opens it goes straight to the item card, as long as they have access to the board.
