Not every response needs to be a full message. Sometimes a "like" is enough, and sometimes you just want to know whether the client or a teammate has already read what you wrote. Three small tools on the item card cover this: reactions, the "Seen by" indicator, and following an item, which decides which updates reach you as notifications.

## What you'll learn

- How to add and remove a reaction to an update
- How to see who viewed an update, when and on which device
- When an update counts as "seen"
- How to follow an item and when following starts automatically

## Reactions to updates

Every update and reply has a row of reactions at the bottom.

1. Click the smiley button (**Add reaction**) under the update.
2. Choose one of the six reactions: **Like**, **Love**, **Haha**, **Celebrate**, **Wow** or **Sad**.
3. The reaction appears as a small chip with a count. The chip of a reaction you chose is highlighted in color.

To remove a reaction, click its chip again. If someone else has already chosen a reaction, you can click its chip to join in, without opening the picker.

![The reaction row under an update, with the reaction picker open](images/seen-reactions-1.webp)

> **Tip:** A reaction is a quiet way to confirm you've seen something, without filling the thread with "thanks" and "got it" replies.

## Who saw the update

In the corner of every update and reply, next to the **Reply** button, there's an eye icon, and next to it up to three profile pictures of people who have already seen the update. If more people saw it, a "+" with the extra number appears too.

1. Hover over the eye icon, or tap it on a phone.
2. The **Seen by** list opens with the number of viewers.
3. Each person has a picture and name, and below them the devices they viewed from: **Computer**, **Phone** or **Tablet**, the browser and operating system, and when they last viewed it.

![The open "Seen by" list with the viewers and their devices](images/seen-reactions-2.webp)

### When an update counts as "seen"

- At least half of the update was visible on the screen for about a second, while the browser tab was active. Scrolling quickly past an update doesn't count.
- An update read in the inbox counts as well.
- Your views of updates you wrote yourself don't count.
- The list updates in real time. When someone opens the item, their picture appears for you within seconds.

> **Note:** "Seen" means the update was shown on the person's screen, not necessarily that they read it to the end. When you need explicit confirmation, ask for a reaction or a reply.

## Following an item

Following decides whether you get a notification about every new update or reply on the item, even when you weren't mentioned.

### Start or stop following

1. Open the item card.
2. In the header, click **Follow**. The button changes to **Following**, and from now on new updates on the item reach you as notifications.
3. To stop, click **Following**.

You can do the same from the board: in the item's menu in the table, choose **Follow item**.

### When following starts on its own

The system adds you as a follower automatically when:

- You created the item
- You were assigned to the item in a people column
- You wrote an update or reply on the item
- You were mentioned in an update on the item

If you stopped following manually, the system remembers your choice and won't add you back automatically.

| What happens on the item | Who gets a notification |
| --- | --- |
| You were mentioned | You, always (according to your notification settings) |
| Someone replied to your update | You, as the update's author |
| A new update was written | Everyone following the item |

How the notification reaches you, in the bell, by email or on your phone, is set in your notification settings. Updates on items you follow are also gathered in the inbox.

## Frequently asked questions

**I get too many notifications from a certain item. What do I do?**\
Open the item and click **Following** to stop following it. You'll keep getting notifications only when someone mentions you directly. To reduce notifications from a whole board, use the board's notification settings.

**Why don't I see myself in the "Seen by" list?**\
An author's views of their own update don't count.

**Who sees the reactions?**\
Everyone who can see the item sees the reaction chips and the count for each. The reactions you chose are highlighted for you.
