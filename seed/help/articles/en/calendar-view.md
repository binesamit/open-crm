The calendar view places each item on its day, based on a date or timeline column on the board. It's perfect for meetings, deliveries, deadlines and publications — anything where you need to see "what happens when". An item with a timeline stretches across all of its days, and you can move items between days with a simple drag.

## What you'll learn

- How to create a calendar view and choose the date column
- How to move between months and weeks
- How to move an item to another day
- How to color the items and what the view looks like on a phone

## Creating a calendar view

1. At the top of the board, click **+** next to the view tabs.
2. Choose **Calendar**, give the view a name and click **OK**.
3. The items appear right away, based on the board's first date column.

> **Note:** The calendar needs a **Date** or **Timeline** column. If there isn't one, you'll see the message **A calendar needs a date or timeline column on the board.**

## Getting around the calendar

At the top of the view you'll find:

- **Previous** and **Next** — go to the previous or next month (or week).
- **Today** — jump straight back to today's date.
- The **Month** / **Week** switch — toggle between the monthly and weekly view.

In the monthly view each day shows up to four items. If there are more, a link such as **+3 more** appears — click it to see all of that day's items. In the weekly view each day has more room, so you almost always see all the items.

Weekend days have a subtle background, based on the account settings, and the first day of the week is also set by the account settings.

## Opening and moving items

- **Open an item:** Clicking an item on the calendar opens the item card.
- **Change a date:** Drag the item to another day. The date in the column updates on the board immediately.
- **An item with a timeline:** When you drag it, the whole item moves and keeps its length — if it lasted three days, it still lasts three days from the new day.

> **Tip:** Every move is recorded in the board's activity log, and you can undo it like any other edit.

## Calendar settings

In the toolbar, click **View settings**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Date column** | Chooses which date or timeline column places the items |
| **Color by** | Chooses a status column whose colors are used for the items on the calendar |

An item with no value in the color column is shown in a neutral color. This way you can, for example, see at a glance which deliveries are "Done" and which are "Stuck".

Settings changes are kept as a draft until you click **Save to view**.

## The calendar on your phone

On a phone the calendar is shown in a compact form: each day has small dots in the items' colors, and below the month you see the list of items for the selected day (today, when you open it). Tap another day to see its items. A day with no items shows **Nothing on this day**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I filter what appears on the calendar?**\
Yes. Search, **Filter** and the **Person** filter in the toolbar also apply to the calendar. For example: only my tasks, or only items with a certain status.

**I have both a "Due date" and a "Start date" column. Which one is shown?**\
The one selected under **Date column** in the view settings. You can create two calendar views, one for each column.

**An item doesn't appear on the calendar.**\
Check that it has a value in the selected date column and that it isn't hidden by an active filter.
