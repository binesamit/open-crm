The **My work** screen gathers every item assigned to you, or to a team you belong to, from all the boards in the account, and sorts them by due date. That way you don't need to jump between boards to see what's waiting for you today, what's already overdue and what's planned for next week. You can update status, date and people right from the screen, without opening the board.

## What you'll learn

- How to get to the My work screen and how it's organized
- How to update items right from the list
- How to filter and group: hiding done items, subitems, grouping by board
- How to choose which columns count for each board

## Open the screen

- **On a computer:** Click **My work** in the sidebar.
- **On a phone:** Tap **My work** in the bottom navigation bar.

## How the screen is organized

By default, items are grouped by due date, from most urgent to furthest away:

| Group | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Items whose due date has already passed. The group is marked in red |
| **Today** | Items due today |
| **This week** | The rest of the current week |
| **Next week** | Items for next week |
| **Later** | Everything further out |
| **No date** | Items without a due date |

Next to each group's name is the number of items in it, and clicking the heading collapses or expands it. On a computer, each row shows **Item**, **Board**, **Status**, **Date** and **People**. A subitem appears with its parent item's name before it, in gray, so it's clear where it belongs.

![The My work screen on a computer, grouped by dates](images/my-work-1.webp)

## Working with the items

1. **Open an item:** Click the item's name to open its item card.
2. **Go to the board:** Click the board's name in the row.
3. **Update in place (on a computer):** Click the status, date or people cell and change it just like on the board. The change is saved to the original board right away.

> **Note:** Editing follows each board's permissions. If you don't have edit permission on a board, its cells are shown read-only.

The list refreshes on its own when something new is assigned to you, and when you come back to the browser tab.

## Filtering and grouping

At the top of the screen there are a few toggles:

- **Hide done items** hides items whose status is marked as done, so you can focus on what's still open.
- **Include subitems** also shows subitems assigned to you, not just top-level items.
- **Group by** lets you choose between **By date** (the default) and **By board**, where each board is a separate group.

## Customizing each board

A board can have several people columns, several date columns and several status columns. In Customize you decide which of them counts.

1. Click **Customize** at the top of the screen.
2. The list shows every board that has items of yours. Use the toggle next to its name to choose whether to show it.
3. For each board, choose:

   - **Date column**: the column used to sort the item into the time groups.
   - **Status column**: the status shown in the row.
   - **People column**: which column makes the item "yours". **Any people column** counts an assignment in any of them.
4. You can choose **None** to not show a particular column.
5. Click **Close**. The settings are saved for you only.

![The Customize window with column choices for each board](images/my-work-2.webp)

> **Tip:** If a board has both "Owner" and "Involved", choose "Owner" as the **People column**. That way you'll only see items you're really responsible for.

## On a phone

On a phone, each item is shown as a compact card: the name, and below it the status, date and board. Tapping a card opens the item card, where you can edit all the fields on the **Fields** tab.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't an item assigned to me show up?**\
Check in **Customize** that the board is shown and the right people column is selected. Also make sure **Hide done items** isn't hiding it. If it's a subitem, turn on **Include subitems**.

**I see my team's items. Why?**\
The screen also includes items assigned to a team you belong to, since they're your responsibility too.

**What does the message about more than 1,000 items mean?**\
When there are more than 1,000 items or more than 50 boards, the most recently updated ones are shown. Turn on **Hide done items** or hide boards that aren't relevant.
