The board is the heart of the app: a flexible table where each row is an item (a client, task, request, deal) and each column is a piece of information about that item. Creating a board takes less than a minute. You can start from an empty board and build it up gradually, or start from a ready-made template that already has suitable columns and groups. This article walks through the process from start until the board is ready for work.

## What you'll learn

- How to open the board creation window
- The difference between a main, private and shareable board
- How to create a board from a template
- What to do right after creating it

## Opening the creation window

There are a few ways to create a board:

1. **From the sidebar** — click the **+** next to the **Workspace** heading and choose **New board**.
2. **Inside a folder** — open the folder's **⋯** menu and choose **New board here**. The board is created directly in the folder.
3. **From the workspace page** — click **New board here** at the top of the page.

![The add menu in the sidebar with the New board and Board from a template options](images/create-first-board-1.webp)

> **Note:** Admins and team members can create boards. If you don't see the **+** button, your role in the account may be viewer or guest.

## Filling in the board details

In the **New board** window:

1. **Board name** — give it a clear name, for example "Leads 2026" or "Service requests". If you leave it empty, the board is created with a default name you can change later.
2. **Template** — leave **Empty board**, or choose a template from the list.
3. **Folder** — choose a folder in the workspace, or leave **No folder**.
4. **Kind** — choose the board's access type (details in the table).
5. Click **Create board**. The board opens right away.

![The new board window with a name, template, folder and board kind](images/create-first-board-2.webp)

### Board kinds

| Kind | Who sees the board | When to choose it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main — everyone** | Everyone who sees the workspace | Most of the team's boards |
| **Private — invited only** | Only people invited to the board | Sensitive information, such as salaries or recruiting |
| **Shareable — incl. guests** | Only people who were invited, including guests from outside the company | Working together with clients or suppliers |

> **Tip:** You can change the board kind after creating it too, in the board's permissions window.

## Creating a board from a template

1. Click the **+** next to **Workspace** and choose **Board from a template**.
2. The window opens with a template already selected. In **Template**, choose the template you want.
3. Fill in the name, folder and kind, and click **Create board**.

Templates include the ready-made structure (columns, groups and settings), so you can start working right away. You can also save an existing board as a template (from its **⋯** menu in the sidebar → **Save as template**), so the whole team works with the same structure.

## After creating it: first steps on the board

An empty board is created with one group and a name column. From here you build:

1. **Adding items** — click the **+ Add item** row at the bottom of the group, type a name and press Enter.
2. **Adding columns** — click **Add column** in the table header and choose a type: status, people, date, numbers and more.
3. **Groups** — add a **New group** to split the board into stages, months or teams.
4. **Renaming** — click the board name at the top of the page to edit it.
5. **Favorites** — star the board in the sidebar to get to it quickly.
6. **Inviting people** — through **⋯** in the board header → **Permissions**, you can add subscribers and invite new people directly to the board.

> **Tip:** Whoever creates the board automatically becomes its owner. Board owners set its permissions and can add more owners.

## FAQ

**I created a board in the wrong folder. How do I move it?**\
Drag it in the sidebar to the right folder, or choose **Move to…** in the board's **⋯** menu.

**Can I duplicate an existing board instead of creating a new one?**\
Yes. In the board's **⋯** menu in the sidebar, choose **Duplicate board**.

**I've reached the board limit. What do I do?**\
The account's plan sets how many boards you can create. The account admin can check usage and upgrade on the **My plan** screen.
