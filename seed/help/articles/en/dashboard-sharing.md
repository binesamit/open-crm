A good dashboard is worth much more when the right people see it. This article shows how to set the dashboard type (main, private or shareable), how to invite people at different permission levels, including guests from outside the company, and how to add a **Dashboard** view of its own to a board, which summarizes only that board and is saved with it.

## What you'll learn

- The difference between a main, private and shareable dashboard
- How to invite people and teams and set permissions
- How to invite guests by email or by link
- How to create a dashboard view inside a board

## Dashboard types

Click **Share** at the top of the dashboard. The button is available to dashboard owners and account admins. In the window, choose the **Dashboard type**:

| Type | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Main** | All workspace members. Each person sees in the widgets only what they're allowed to see on the boards |
| **Private** | Only people who were invited |
| **Shareable** | Only people who were invited, and you can invite guests too. A guest sees in the widgets only boards shared with them |

> **Note:** Sharing the dashboard doesn't open access to the boards themselves. Someone without permission to a board won't see its data in the widgets, even if they were invited to the dashboard.

## Inviting people and setting permissions

1. In the share window, under **Invited**, click **Add a person or team**.
2. Choose a team member or a whole team.
3. For each invitee, set a **Permission**:

   - **Owner** — full control, including sharing and deleting.
   - **Editor** — adding, moving and setting up widgets, and choosing the boards.
   - **Viewer** — view only, including personal filtering.
4. To remove an invitee, click the remove button next to their name.
5. Click **Save**.

## Inviting by email, including guests

In the **Invite by email** area at the bottom of the window:

1. Type email addresses (separated by commas).
2. In **New people join as**, choose **Member** or **Guest**.
3. Choose a **Permission** for the dashboard, and add a **Personal message (optional)**.
4. Choose **Email the invitation**, or **No email — only a link to copy**.
5. Click **Send invitations**.

People already in the account are added to the dashboard right away. New people get an invitation to the account, and are added to the dashboard as soon as they accept it. If you chose no email, personal invitation links appear for you to copy: you can send them by WhatsApp, SMS or any other way.

> **Note:** **Guests can only be invited to a shareable dashboard.** Change the dashboard type first and save. A guest can get at most editor permission.

## A dashboard view inside a board

Sometimes you don't need a separate dashboard, just a summary of one board, right inside it. That's what the **Dashboard** view type is for.

1. At the top of the board, click **+** next to the view tabs.
2. Choose **Dashboard**, give it a name and click **OK**.
3. Click **Add widget** and choose: **Numbers**, **Chart**, **Status battery**, **Items table**, **Calendar**, **Timeline**, **Workload**, **Time report** or **Text**.
4. Set up each widget from its **⋯** menu → **Settings**, exactly as in a regular dashboard, including what to **Count**: items, subitems or both.
5. Click **Save to view** to save the widgets and the layout.

![A dashboard view inside a board, with the board's filter bar and numbers and table widgets](images/dashboard-sharing-2.webp)

### The board's filter applies to the widgets

By default, the board's search, **Filter** and **Person** filter narrow down all the widgets. Above the widgets it says **Widgets follow this view's filter, search and person**. So you can, for example, search for one client and immediately see their numbers.

To make the widgets always show the whole board, open **View settings** and turn off **The view's filter applies to the widgets**. Then it says **Widgets show the whole board**.

> **Tip:** A dashboard view is a view like any other: you can duplicate it, lock it, make it the board's default, or copy it to another board.

## Separate dashboard or dashboard view?

|  | Separate dashboard | Dashboard view on a board |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How many boards | Up to 50 | The current board only |
| Permissions | Its own sharing (main, private, shareable) | The board's permissions |
| **Board view** widget and extension widgets | Yes | No |
| Filtering | The dashboard's filter bar | The board's filter |

## FAQ

**I invited a team member and they see different numbers from me. Why?**\
Each person sees in the widgets only items and boards they're allowed to see. Check their permissions on the boards themselves.

**How do I transfer ownership of a dashboard?**\
In the **Share** window, give another person **Owner** permission. A dashboard can have several owners.

**Can I turn a dashboard view into a separate dashboard?**\
Not directly. Create a **New dashboard** from the sidebar and add the board and widgets to it.
