The sidebar is the starting point for everything you do on the platform: from it you get to the Home screen, your personal work, updates, search and all the boards in the workspace. Knowing it well saves a lot of clicks, especially the favorites: a personal list of the boards, docs and dashboards you open most, always at the top of the sidebar.

## What you'll learn

- What's in each part of the sidebar
- How to add things to your favorites and arrange them
- How to collapse the sidebar into a narrow strip of icons
- How to get around on your phone

## How the sidebar is laid out

![The full sidebar: the account name, the shortcuts row, the favorites and the workspace tree](images/navigation-favorites-1.webp)

From top to bottom:

1. **The account name and your role**: clicking it takes you back to the Home screen.
2. **The shortcuts row**: a row of small icons. Hover over an icon to see its name:

   - **Home**: the account's Home screen.
   - **My work**: all the items assigned to you, from every board.
   - **Inbox**: updates and replies waiting for you; the number of unread ones shows on the icon.
   - **Automations**: the account's automations center.
   - Members and admins also see **Flows** and **Agents**.
   - **Search**: search the whole account (also with the shortcut Ctrl+B).
   - **Help center**: articles and answers to questions.
   - **The bell**: your notifications.
3. **Favorites**: your personal list (more on this below).
4. **Workspace**: the name of the current workspace (click it to switch workspaces), and below it the tree of folders, boards, docs and dashboards. Next to the heading there's a button to collapse or expand all folders, and a **+** button for adding.
5. **Your avatar**: at the bottom of the sidebar. Clicking it opens your personal menu.

### The ⋯ menu on every row

Every board, doc, dashboard and folder in the tree has a **⋯** menu that appears on hover, or when you right-click the row. A board's menu has, for example: add to favorites, **Open in new tab**, **Copy link**, **Rename**, **Move to…**, **Duplicate board**, **Save as template**, **Archive** and **Delete**, depending on your permissions.

### The avatar menu

In the menu that opens from your avatar you'll find:

- **My profile** and **Profile photo**.
- **Appearance**: light/dark mode, color theme and the sidebar's tint.
- **Switch account**: when you're a member of more than one account.
- **Administration**: for admins only.
- **Trash** and **Archive**.
- **Sign out**.

## Favorites

Favorites are a personal list (only you see it) that brings together boards, docs and dashboards from all workspaces.

**Adding to favorites:**

1. Hover over a board in the sidebar and click the star that appears next to it, or
2. Open the board's **⋯** menu and choose **Add to favorites**, or
3. On the workspace page, click the star on the board's card.

**Removing:** Click the filled star again, or choose **Remove from favorites**.

**Arranging:** Drag an entry within the **Favorites** list to change the order. The order is saved and reaches all your devices.

> **Tip:** You can also mark a doc as a favorite from inside the doc itself, and it joins the same list in the sidebar.

## Collapsing the sidebar

When you need more room for a wide board:

1. Click the **Collapse menu** button at the bottom of the sidebar, next to your avatar.
2. The sidebar turns into a narrow strip of large icons. The boards button in the strip opens a panel with the workspace tree.
3. To go back to the full sidebar, click **Expand menu**.

Your choice is remembered on the device you're working on.

## Getting around on your phone

![The bottom navigation bar on a phone with its five tabs](images/navigation-favorites-2.webp)

On a phone the sidebar is hidden, and instead there's:

- **A top bar**: a **Menu** button that opens the sidebar as a drawer, the name of the current screen, search and the bell.
- **A bottom bar** with five tabs:

  - **Home**: the Home screen.
  - **My work**: your items.
  - **Updates**: the inbox.
  - **Boards**: the workspace tree in full screen, with workspace switching and **+** for creating.
  - **Me**: your profile, settings, switching accounts and signing out.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see the "Flows" and "Agents" icons. Why?**\
They're available to admins and members only. Viewers and guests don't see them.

**Are my favorites shared with the team?**\
No. Favorites are completely personal, and every user has their own list.

**A board disappeared from my favorites.**\
If the board was deleted, archived or your permission to it was removed, it no longer appears in the list.
