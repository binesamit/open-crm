The platform is built for full work from your phone too, not just for viewing. You can update statuses, add items, write updates and mention people, switch views and filter a board, all on a screen made for your thumb. This article walks you through the screen's layout and the quick ways to do common actions on it.

## What you'll learn

- How to find your way around the bottom navigation bar and the top bar
- How to work with a board on your phone: views, search, filtering and editing
- How to edit an item from its card
- What's on the "Me" screen

> **Tip:** For an app experience, with an icon on your home screen and push notifications, install the platform to your home screen. The explanation is in the article "Installing the app on your phone and push notifications".

## The bottom navigation bar

At the bottom of the screen there are five fixed tabs. The tab for the screen you're on is highlighted.

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The account's home screen |
| **My work** | All the items assigned to you, by due date |
| **Updates** | The inbox, with a count of what's unread |
| **Boards** | Workspaces, folders and boards |
| **Me** | Your profile, settings, switching accounts and signing out |

![The main screen on a phone with the bottom navigation bar](images/mobile-usage-1.webp)

## The top bar

The top of the screen shows the current screen's name, and next to it:

- **☰ (menu)**: opens the full sidebar, with favorites, workspaces and all the links.
- **Search**: search the whole account.
- **Bell**: your notifications.
- **?**: the help center and opening a support request.

## Working with a board

1. Tap **Boards** and choose a workspace and a board.
2. A single compact bar appears at the top of the screen: the board's name with the current view's name below it, and next to them the search, **Filter & view**, **⋯** and **+** buttons.
3. The board's description and the views row scroll together with the rows, so most of the screen is free for data.

### Switch views

- The views row above the table shows all the board's views. Tap a view to switch to it.
- Tapping the board's name in the top bar opens the **Views** list from the bottom. There you can choose a view, open its **⋯** menu (pin, rename, delete) or create a **New view** with **+**.

### Search and filter

- Tap the search icon. The search field opens over the bar and results are filtered as you type. When you close the field, the search stays active, and a dot on the icon reminds you.
- Tap **Filter & view** to open all the board tools: **Filter**, **Sort**, **Group by**, **Hide** and **View settings**. When filter conditions are active, a count appears on the button, and below the bar each condition is shown as a tag with an X for quick removal.

![The compact board bar on a phone and the "Filter & view" window open](images/mobile-usage-2.webp)

### Editing in the table

On a phone the board is shown as a compact table: the name column stays fixed at the side, and the other columns scroll sideways.

1. **Edit a value:** Tap a cell. The column's editor opens as a panel from the bottom of the screen, for example a status picker, a calendar or a list of people.
2. **Open an item:** Tap the item's name to open its card.
3. **Add an item:** Tap **+** in the top bar, or the add row at the end of each group.
4. **Subitems:** The arrow next to the item's name opens its subitems right in the table.

> **Tip:** Prefer cards to a table? In **Filter & view** → **View settings**, under **Table on phones**, choose **Cards**. Each item is shown as a card with its name and the important columns.

## The item card on a phone

On a phone the item card opens full screen, with a back arrow in the corner.

- The **Updates** tab works as it does on a computer: writing, mentioning with @, attaching photos from the camera or gallery, reactions and replies.
- The **Fields** tab is the convenient way to edit an item. All the fields are arranged one below the other.
- To go back to the board, tap the back arrow.

![A full-screen item card on a phone, on the Fields tab](images/mobile-usage-3.webp)

## The "Me" screen

The **Me** tab gathers everything related to you:

- **My profile**: **Edit profile**, **Notification settings**, **API tokens** and **Appearance** (light, dark or following the system).
- **Profile photo**: upload or remove your photo.
- **Settings and tools**: **Administration** (for admins), **Automations**, **Flows**, **Trash** and **Archive**. Some links appear only depending on your role.
- **Switch account**: if you're a member of more than one account.
- **Sign out** of the platform.

## Tips for daily work

- Start the day in **My work**: all your tasks in one place, sorted by urgency.
- Go through **Updates** to reply to mentions. You can reply right from the inbox.
- Tap a phone number in a phone column to call, send a WhatsApp message or an SMS right from the board.

## Frequently asked questions

**Some columns are missing from the table on my phone.**\
The columns scroll sideways. Swipe the table left or right. If the column is hidden in the view, check **Filter & view** → **Hide**.

**How do I get to the full sidebar on my phone?**\
Tap **☰** in the corner of the top bar.

**Can I work on my phone without installing?**\
Yes. All the features are available in the browser too. Installing adds an icon to your home screen, full screen and push notifications.
