The updates tab in the item card is where the conversation around the work happens. Instead of correspondence scattered across email and WhatsApp, all the decisions, questions and files are kept right next to the item itself, and anyone who joins later sees the whole history. With @ mentions, the right people get notified exactly when they're needed.

## What you'll learn

- How to write an update and format it
- How to attach files, images and screenshots
- How to mention a teammate or an AI agent
- How to reply to, edit, copy and delete updates
- How unsent drafts work

## Write an update

1. Open the item card. The **Updates** tab opens first.
2. Click in the **Write an update… (@ to mention)** box and type your text.
3. Click **Update** to post it, or press **Ctrl+Enter** (on a Mac: ⌘+Enter).

> **Tip:** Enter on its own starts a new line and doesn't send the update, so you can write several paragraphs at your own pace. The reminder **Enter for a new line · Ctrl+Enter to send** appears below the box.

The update appears at the top of the list with your name and a relative time (for example "5 min ago"). Hovering over the time shows the exact date and time. The count next to the tab's name, and the bubble in the item's row on the board, update immediately.

## Format the text

The **Text formatting** bar above the box has six tools at hand: **Bold**, **Italic**, **Bulleted list**, **Link**, **Mention** and **Emoji**. Everything else is in the **More** menu:

| In the "More" menu | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, **Highlight** | Making important words stand out |
| **Heading**, **Subheading** | Splitting a long update into sections |
| **Numbered list**, **Checklist** | Steps, or tasks with checkboxes |
| **Quote**, **Code**, **Divider** | A quote from a client, a technical snippet, separating topics |

The familiar keyboard shortcuts work here too, for example Ctrl+B for bold and Ctrl+I for italic.

> **Tip:** In an update that includes a **Checklist**, the update's author can tick ✓ the boxes right in the posted update, without opening it for editing.

## Attach files and images

There are three ways to attach files to an update:

1. Click **Attach a file** at the bottom of the box and choose files.
2. Drag files from your computer into the box.
3. Paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot or image right into the box.

While uploading, **Uploading…** appears. You can remove a file you attached by mistake before sending by clicking its X. You can send an update that's only files, with no text.

In a posted update, images appear as thumbnails and other files appear with their name and size. Clicking a file opens the file viewer, where you can page through the files, zoom into an image, **Download** and write **Comments on the file**. The update's author, the board owners and account admins can also **Remove file**.

## Mention people

1. Inside the update, type **@** (or click **Mention** in the bar).
2. Start typing a name. A list of matching account members appears.
3. Choose a person with the mouse, or with the arrows and Enter.

Whoever is mentioned gets a notification and starts following the item, so later updates reach them too. Once you start typing a name, the mention list also shows AI agents working in the account, marked **Digital worker**. Mentioning an agent asks it to handle a task. An agent that isn't available right now appears in gray with the reason, and you can't choose it.

## Threaded replies

1. Below the update, click **Reply**.
2. Write in the **Write a reply…** box and click **Reply** (or Ctrl+Enter).

You can also reply to a reply. Replies are indented so it's clear what each one answers, up to five levels deep. The author of the original update is notified of every reply.

## Edit, copy and delete

Every update and reply has a **⋯** menu (**Update actions**). You can also open it by right-clicking the update:

- **Reply** opens a reply box.
- **Edit** is available to the author only. After saving, the mark **edited** appears next to the time. Anyone mentioned for the first time in the edit gets a notification.
- **Copy text** copies the update's content.
- **Delete** is available to the author, the board owners and account admins. The platform asks for confirmation (**Delete this update?**), and the update goes to the Trash. In the message that appears you can click **Undo** to bring it back immediately.

## Unsent drafts

Started writing and closed the card? The text isn't lost. When you open the item again, the draft is waiting in the box marked **Saved draft**, with **Clear** next to it if you want to start over. Drafts are saved in the browser you wrote in, for 12 hours, separately for each item and each reply.

> **Note:** Users with the "Viewer" role can read updates but not write them. If the writing box doesn't appear for you, contact the account admin.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why isn't the update sent when I press Enter?**\
Enter starts a new line. To send, click **Update** or press Ctrl+Enter.

**Can I mention someone who isn't a member of the account?**\
No. The list shows only active account members. To share with someone external, first invite them to the account, for example as a guest.

**I deleted an update by mistake. What do I do?**\
Click **Undo** in the message that appears right after deleting, or restore it from the Trash.
