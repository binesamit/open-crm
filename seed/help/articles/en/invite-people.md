A work platform is worth much more when the whole team works in it together. Access to Bines OS is by invitation only, so every new person — employee, partner or client — joins through a personal invitation. You can invite from several places: the home screen, a specific board, a workspace or administration. In every case the invitation is sent by email, and you can also copy the link and send it yourself on WhatsApp, by SMS or any other way.

## What you'll learn

- How to invite people from the home screen
- How to send an invitation as a link, without email
- How to invite directly to a board or workspace
- How to track invitations, resend them and revoke them

## Before you start: which role to choose?

| Role | Suitable for |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Whoever manages the account: users, settings, security and plan |
| **Member** | Employees who create and edit boards and items |
| **Viewer** | Anyone who needs to see information without changing it |
| **Guest** | People outside the organization — clients, suppliers — who work with you on shared boards only |

> **Note:** Admins can invite any role. Members can invite viewers and guests, depending on the account settings. Viewers and guests don't invite new people.

## Invite from the home screen

1. Click **Home** in the sidebar.
2. In the **Invite users** card, type one or more addresses in **Email addresses**, separated by commas.
3. Choose a **Role**.
4. Make sure the switch shows **Email the invitation**. If you'd rather send it yourself, turn it off — it will show **No email — only a link to copy**.
5. Click **Send invitations**.

A message appears with the number of invitations sent, and below it a personal link for each invitee, with **Copy** and **WhatsApp** buttons.

![The Invite users card on the home screen, with the links created after sending](images/invite-people-1.webp)

> **Tip:** The email didn't reach the invitee? Send them the link yourself. The link is personal, single-use and valid for a number of days set in the account settings.

If one of the addresses wasn't accepted, the message gives the reason next to it — for example that the person is already a member of the account, or that you've reached the number of users in the plan.

## Invite directly to a board

When you invite from inside a board, the invitee is added to that board as soon as they accept the invitation — no need to add them afterwards.

1. In the board's header, click **⋯** and choose **Permissions**.
2. Scroll to the **Invite by email** section (or click the **Invite by email** button next to the subscribers list).
3. Fill in addresses, choose a role and click **Send invitations**.

Anyone already in the account is added to the board immediately. Guests can be invited only to a **Shareable** board — if the board is main or private, a message explains how to change its type.

## Invite to a workspace

1. Open the workspace page (in the workspace's **⋯** menu in the sidebar → **Workspace page**).
2. In the **Workspace members** section, use **Invite by email**.

Anyone already in the account is added immediately; someone new gets an invitation to the account and joins the workspace when they accept it.

## What the invitee sees

The invitee gets an email with a link. The link opens a join screen showing the account's name and the role. Someone new to the platform picks a name and password and clicks **Choose a password and join**; someone who already has a user signs in and clicks **Join the account**. Invitations not yet accepted also appear for the invitee on the account picker screen, under **Pending invitations**.

## Tracking invitations (for account admins)

Account admins see pending invitations in the invitation card on the home screen, and the full picture in administration:

1. Open the avatar menu and choose **Administration**, then **Users**.
2. In the list of open invitations, each invitation has a status: **Emailed**, **Opened**, **Link only**, **Email failed** or **Expired** — along with who invited and until when the invitation is valid.
3. For each invitation you can:

   - **Resend** — send the email again (or **Send again, valid for…** to choose a different validity).
   - **Copy link** — create a new link to send manually. The previous link stops working.
   - **Revoke** — cancel the invitation; the link stops working immediately.

### Invitation settings

On the same screen, in the **Invitation settings** section, admins set rules for all invitations in the account:

- **Link valid for (days)** — between 1 and 90. Resending renews the validity.
- **Wait between emails (minutes)** — how long to wait before sending the same invitation again.
- **Tell the inviter when the invitation is accepted**.

## Frequently asked questions

**The "Guest" option shows "Not in the plan" for me.**\
Inviting guests depends on the account's plan. The account admin can check this on the **My plan** screen.

**I invited the wrong address by mistake. What do I do?**\
The account admin can revoke the invitation from the list of open invitations. After revoking, the link won't work.

**Can I change the role after the invitee has joined?**\
Yes. The account admin changes roles on the users screen in administration.
