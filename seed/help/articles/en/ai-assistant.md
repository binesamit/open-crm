The AI assistant is a chat window that opens next to the board or the item card. You can ask it questions about what's in front of you ("What is late?"), ask for a summary, a draft update or ideas for next steps, and even ask it to make changes, such as creating follow-up tasks. The assistant always works with your permissions, and no change happens before you've explicitly approved it.

## What you'll learn

- How to open the AI assistant on a board and in an item card
- How to ask questions and request summaries
- How to approve, reject and undo changes the assistant suggests
- How to go back to earlier conversations and start a new one
- What to do when the assistant isn't available

## Opening the assistant

1. Go to the board. In the board header, next to the automations button, click **AI assistant** (the sparkle icon).
2. The assistant window opens at the side of the screen. The board name appears at its top; that's the context the assistant is talking about.
3. To chat about a specific item, open the item card and click the sparkle icon at the top of the card. In that case the assistant focuses on the item: its fields, updates and files.

![The AI assistant window open next to a board, with suggestions for first questions](images/ai-assistant-1.webp)

> **Tip:** When the conversation is empty, the assistant shows a few ready-made suggestions. On a board: **Summarize this board**, **What is late?**, **Create 5 follow-up tasks**. On an item: **Summarize this item**, **What is the next step?**, **Draft a status update**. Clicking a suggestion sends it right away.

## Questions, summaries and answers

1. Write your question or request in the box at the bottom of the window (**Ask or request anything…**).
2. Click **Send** or press Enter. To start a new line without sending, use Shift+Enter.
3. The answer is written as you watch, and above it small tags show what the assistant read in order to answer, for example **Read the board**, **Search items**, **Updates** or **Files**. That way it's always clear what the answer is based on.

The assistant can search everything you're allowed to see in the account: boards, items, updates, files, "My work", automations and their run history, time entries and the activity log. It doesn't see anything that's blocked for you.

## Approving changes before they happen

When you ask the assistant to do something (create items, update values, move an item to a group, write an update and so on), it doesn't do it right away. Instead, each action is shown as a card with a clear description of what's about to happen (for example: create item "Price quote" on board "Clients"), and the status **Waiting for approval**.

1. Read the description on each card.
2. Click **Approve** to carry out the action, or **Reject** to skip it.
3. When several actions are waiting, you can approve them all at once by clicking **Approve all**.
4. After they run, a message "N actions done" appears with an **Undo** button. All the actions approved together are undone together, with one click.

![An assistant action card with Approve and Reject buttons](images/ai-assistant-2.webp)

> **Note:** Deleting an item through the assistant moves it to the Trash, and you can restore it from there. The cost of the message is also shown at the bottom of each answer.

## Earlier conversations

Conversations are saved separately for each person, for each board and each item. When you open the assistant again in the same place, the last conversation continues.

- Click **Earlier conversations** at the top of the window to see the list of conversations and choose one.
- Click **New conversation** to start from scratch.
- To delete a conversation, click the delete icon next to it and confirm. The conversation is deleted only from your history.

## When the assistant isn't available

If a message appears instead of the chat, it explains why:

| The message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| AI is off in this account. | The account admin hasn't turned on AI yet. |
| The AI assistant is off in this account. | AI is on, but the assistant itself is off. |
| No AI connection is chosen for the assistant yet. | An AI connection needs to be chosen in the settings. |
| The AI assistant is not available for your role in this account. | The settings allow the assistant only for some roles. |

Account admins see an **Open AI & MCP settings** link that leads straight to the right setting; other users can contact their account admin. If the button itself appears grayed out, AI may not be included in the account's plan.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the assistant change something without my knowing?**\
No. Every change is shown as a card and waits for your approval, and even after approving you can undo all the actions together.

**Does the assistant see boards I don't have access to?**\
No. The assistant works with exactly your permissions. A guest, for example, gets answers only about what they themselves can see.

**I got a message that the assistant has reached its limit. What do I do?**\
The account admin sets how many messages a day each person may send, and a cost cap per message. You can try again tomorrow or ask the admin to raise the limit.
