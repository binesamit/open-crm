When something isn't clear, the answer is usually a click away. The help center is available from every screen in the app, and shows first the articles related to what you're doing right now: on a board you get articles about boards and views, and in an item card articles about updates and fields. If you don't find what you need, you can ask the help assistant in your own words, and if that's not enough either, open a ticket with the support team without losing what you've already written.

## What you'll learn

- How to open the help center from inside the app
- How to find articles that match the current screen, and how to search
- How to ask the help assistant
- How to reach the public help center without signing in

## Opening the help center

1. Click **?** (**Help**). On a computer it's at the top of the sidebar, next to the bell. On a phone it's in the top bar.
2. In the menu that opens, choose **Help center**.
3. A help panel opens over the screen you're on, so you can read and get straight back to work.

The same menu also has **Open a ticket** and **My tickets**. They're explained in the article "Contacting support".

![The help menu open with Help center, Open a ticket and My tickets](images/help-center-1.webp)

## Articles for the current screen

When the panel opens and the search field is empty, **Articles for this page** are shown: articles chosen especially for the screen you're on. So without typing a word, you'll see, for example, articles about filters and views on a board, and articles about building an automation on the automations screen.

If **No articles for this page yet** appears, just search.

## Searching articles

1. Type a word or a question in the **Search help articles** field, for example "how do I mute a board".
2. The results update as you type. Search understands Hebrew, including different forms of the same word.
3. Click an article to read it inside the panel. **Back** takes you back to the list.

If nothing is found, the message **No articles found** appears, with a suggestion to try other words, ask the assistant or open a ticket.

### "Did this help?"

At the end of every article, the question **Did this help?** appears with **Yes** and **No**. Every answer helps us improve the articles. Clicking **No** takes you back to the list so you can try another article.

![The help panel with search results and an open article](images/help-center-2.webp)

## Asking the help assistant

When you search, the **Ask the assistant** button appears under the results. The assistant is an AI that reads the help articles and writes a focused answer to your question.

1. Write your question in the search field, in your own words. For example: "How do I get a daily summary by email instead of an email for every notification?".
2. Click **Ask the assistant**.
3. The answer appears with the caption **From:** and the list of articles it's based on. Clicking an article opens it.
4. If the assistant didn't find an answer in the articles, it says so and suggests opening a ticket.

> **Note:** The assistant answers only from published help articles. It doesn't see the data in your account and doesn't take actions. For help working on the boards themselves, use the app's "AI assistant".

The assistant is available when the account's plan includes AI and the account admin hasn't turned it off. If monthly usage has reached its cap, the message **The assistant is not available right now. You can open a ticket.** appears.

## Still need help?

At the bottom of the panel there's an **Open a ticket** button. What you typed in the search moves to the ticket form, so you don't need to write everything again.

## The public help center

There is also a full help center open to everyone, without signing in, at the app's address with `/help` at the end. There the articles are arranged by category, and there's a search. It's the place to send a colleague who hasn't signed up yet, or to read when you don't have access to your account. Some articles, for example articles for admins, are shown only to people who are signed in.

## For account admins

In **Administration** → **Security**, in the **Help center assistant** section, you set:

- Whether the **Help assistant on in this account** is turned on.
- Which **Connection** answers questions: **The platform's** (up to a monthly cap) or **The account's own**, meaning an AI connection the account set up. In that case, usage counts toward the account's AI budget.

## FAQ

**I don't see the "?" button.**\
Guests don't see the help menu. They can use the public help center at `/help`.

**Why doesn't the "Ask the assistant" button appear?**\
The button appears only after you type a question in the search, and only when the assistant is available in the account.

**Are the articles available in English?**\
Translated articles show a link to the version in the other language at the top of the article.
