# Trademark policy

The source code is free software under the AGPL-3.0 (`LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md`). The **name** "Bines OS"
and the **logo** of the product are not: they belong to Amit Bines and identify the original
project, and this policy says how they may be used. The policy follows the practice of other open-source projects (for
example those of the Linux Foundation, Grafana and Twenty).

## You may, without asking

- Use the name in text to refer to the software: "built on Bines OS", "compatible with Bines OS", "an extension for
  Bines OS", "imported from Bines OS", in documentation, articles, talks, comparisons and reviews.
- Run an unmodified copy, or a copy with only configuration and branding changes made through the settings the
  software provides, and call it by its name — for your own organisation or as a service for others.
- Use the name and the logo to link to the official project site or repository.
- Distribute unmodified copies (source or the official container images) under the name.

## You must

- Use your own product name and logo for a **modified** version of the software that you distribute or run as a
  service for others. The product name shown to users is a setting, so this needs no code change. You may state that
  your product is "based on Bines OS" or "a fork of Bines OS".
- Not use the name, or anything confusingly similar, as the name of your company, product, service, domain, app-store
  listing, social-media handle or extension.
- Not use the logo except to link to the project, and never in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership or official
  status.
- Not register the name or the logo, or confusingly similar marks, as a trademark, domain or company name anywhere.

## Extensions and integrations

Name your extension "<your name> for Bines OS" or "<your name> (Bines OS extension)"; do not name it "Bines OS <something>"
as if it were part of the product. The extension catalog may show the product's logo next to the word "compatible"
only in the layout the catalog itself provides.

## Questions

Open a discussion in the project's repository. Anything this policy does not clearly permit needs written permission
from Amit Bines.
