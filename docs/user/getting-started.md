# Getting started

Hebrew version: [getting-started.he.md](getting-started.he.md)

## What it's for

Bines OS keeps your work in boards: lists of items (tasks, clients, projects, orders) with columns you choose. This
guide covers signing in, finding your way around, and setting up workspaces, folders and boards. It also covers using
Bines OS on a phone.

## Sign in

1. Open the address of your Bines OS installation.
2. Enter your "Email" and "Password" and press "Sign in".
3. Other ways to sign in may show on the same screen, depending on how your account is set up:
   - "Email me a sign-in link": a one-time link is sent to your mailbox.
   - "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Microsoft".

Access is by invitation. If you got an invitation email, open its link and choose "Choose a password and join", or
"I already have a user — sign in".

Forgot your password? Press "Forgot password" and then "Send reset link". The link is valid for one hour. If email
does not reach you, an account admin can give you a one-time sign-in link from the users page.

To sign out: open your avatar menu (your photo or initials) and choose "Sign out".

## Switch between accounts

One user can belong to several accounts (for example, your own business and a client's).

- After signing in with more than one account, you get "Choose an account". Pick one.
- To switch later: avatar menu > "Switch account", then pick the account. Your role in each account is shown next to
  its name.

## Find your way around

- The sidebar holds the workspace switcher, the board tree (folders, boards, docs and dashboards), "Favorites", and
  links such as "Home", "My work" and "Inbox".
- The avatar menu holds "My profile", "Appearance", "Switch account", "Trash", "Archive" and "Sign out". Admins also
  see "Administration".
- To collapse the sidebar, press "Collapse menu". Press "Expand menu" to open it again.
- Press Shift+? at any time to see the "Keyboard shortcuts". Ctrl+B searches everything.

### Choose what shows in the sidebar and in the top bar

1. Open "My profile" and go to the "Appearance" tab.
2. Under "Navigation: sidebar and top bar", choose "The account’s default" or "My own layout".
3. With your own layout, pick a "Quick layout" ("Everything in the sidebar", "Navigation on top" or "Custom"), or
   choose for each item whether it sits on the side, on top, or is hidden.

If an admin turned on "Apply to everyone", the layout is the same for all members and can't be changed in your
profile.

## The Home page

"Home" welcomes you to the account. It lists the account's "Workspaces" (each opens its page) and a quick way to
invite people. Admins also get a shortcut card, "Open administration".

## Workspaces

A workspace groups related folders and boards, for example "Sales" or "Operations".

### Switch workspace

1. At the top of the sidebar, open the workspace switcher ("Switch workspace").
2. Pick a workspace.

### Create a workspace

Admins and members can create workspaces.

1. Open the workspace switcher.
2. Choose "+ New workspace" and type the "Workspace name".

### Open or closed

On the workspace page ("Workspace page"), under "Access":

- "Open workspace": everyone in the account sees the workspace; each board follows its own permissions.
- "Closed workspace": only the "Workspace members" and account admins see it. Use "Add member" to add people. People
  already in the account join right away; new people get an invitation.

The workspace page also has a "Description" and shows the boards as "Cards" or a "List".

### Delete a workspace

Only a workspace without boards can be deleted. Its folders are deleted with it. Use the workspace menu > "Delete
workspace".

## Folders

Folders keep the board tree tidy. They nest up to three levels.

- To create one: "Add new" > "New folder", or a folder's menu > "New sub-folder".
- To move a board, doc or dashboard into a folder: drag it onto the folder, or use its menu > "Move to…". Drop it on
  the area marked "Drop here to take it out of a folder" to take it out.
- "Collapse all folders" and "Expand all folders" open and close the whole tree.

### Delete a folder

1. Open the folder's menu and choose "Delete".
2. If the folder holds boards, choose what happens to them:
   - "Move the boards to the root": only the folder is deleted.
   - "Delete the boards with the folder": everything goes to trash. Restoring the folder brings the boards back.

Docs and dashboards move to the root either way.

## Boards

### Create a board

1. In the sidebar, press "Add new" and choose "New board" (or "Board from a template"). From a folder's menu you can
   also choose "New board here".
2. Fill in the "Board name".
3. Optional: pick a "Folder" and a "Template" (or keep "Empty board").
4. Choose the "Type":
   - "Main — everyone": everyone in the account.
   - "Private — invited only": only people invited to the board.
   - "Shareable — incl. guests": like private, and guests from outside the account can be invited.
5. Press "Create board".

An empty board offers "Add a first item" or "Templates".

Admins also see "Start from a full template or with AI" in the "Add new" menu.

### Board actions in the sidebar

Each board's menu (the ⋯ next to its name) has:

- "Add to favorites" / "Remove from favorites"
- "Open in new tab", "Copy link", "Copy ID"
- "Rename", "Move to…", "Move to workspace…"
- "Duplicate board", "Save as template"
- "Archive", "Delete"

What you see depends on your permissions on the board. Moving a main board from a closed workspace into an open one
makes it visible to everyone in the account. You are asked to confirm first.

### Favorites

Favorites are personal. Mark a board, doc or dashboard with "Add to favorites" and it shows under "Favorites" at the
top of the sidebar.

### Board description

Press "Add a description" under the board name to say what the board is for, who it is for and how to work in it.

## Your profile, language and look

Open the avatar menu > "My profile".

- "Profile" tab: your "Full name", "Job title", "Department / team", "Phone", "Birthday", "Language" and "Time zone".
  These are the same in every account. Pick "Language" to switch the interface between Hebrew and English.
- "Sign-in & security" tab: set or change your password.
- "Notifications" tab: what you are told about, and where. See
  [Notifications and inbox](notifications-and-inbox.md).
- "Appearance" tab: "Light", "Dark" or "System", the "Colour theme", and the navigation layout.

To change your photo, click your avatar on the profile page: "Upload profile photo", "Choose an avatar" or "Remove
photo" (PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 5 MB).

## On a phone

Bines OS works in the phone's browser. Phones get a layout of their own:

- A bottom bar with "Home", "My work", "Updates", "Boards" and "Me".
- On a board, items show as a list. The top bar has "Views" to switch views and "Filter & view" for filters and view
  settings. Renaming the board, adding a group and other actions are in the ⋯ menu.
- Some tools need a computer, for example the "Import" sheet.

### Add Bines OS to your home screen

If your account admin turned it on, a bar offers "Add Bines OS to your home screen?" (the name may be your account's).
Press "Install". It then opens like an app, without the address bar. "Not now" hides the bar for a month.

On iPhone:

1. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
2. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
3. Tap "Add". The icon appears on your home screen.

### Phone notifications

To get notifications on your phone even when the app is closed, open "My profile" > "Notifications" and, under
"Phone", press "Turn on for this device". Then tick, in the "Phone" column, which notifications should reach the
phone. On iPhone, install the app to the home screen first. Admins can turn phone
notifications off for the whole account.

## When the connection drops

If your internet connection drops, a banner says "No internet connection. Changes won’t be saved until it’s back."
Wait for the connection to return before you keep editing.

When a new version is out, a bar says "A new version is available". Press "Refresh". Everything you saved stays.
