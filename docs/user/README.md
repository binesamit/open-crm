# Bines OS user guide

Hebrew version: [README.he.md](README.he.md)

This guide is for the people who work in Bines OS every day: managers, team members and anyone who was invited to a
board. Each page covers one area of the product, with short step-by-step tasks. You don't need any technical
knowledge.

Running your own server? See the [installation and licensing guide](../GUIDE.md). Building on top of Bines OS? See
the [API reference](../06-API.md) and the [extensions guide](../EXTENSIONS-GUIDE.md).

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): signing in, accounts, workspaces, folders, boards, the home page and using
  Bines OS on a phone.
- [Boards, groups and items](boards-groups-items.md): working with groups, items and subitems, moving and
  duplicating, editing many items at once, undo and the trash.
- [Columns](columns.md): every column type in plain words, formulas, connecting boards, mirror columns, buttons and
  computed names.
- [Views](views.md): table, Kanban, Gantt, timeline, calendar, map, files, chart, form and more.
- [Search, filter, sort and group](search-filter-sort-group.md): finding things quickly and shaping what a view shows.

## Working together

- [The item card and working together](item-card-and-collaboration.md): updates, mentions, replies, files and file
  versions, the activity log and the item menu.
- [Notifications, inbox and My work](notifications-and-inbox.md): the bell, the inbox, My work, notification
  settings and sending email into an item.
- [Docs and forms](docs-and-forms.md): shared docs that update live, and forms that turn answers into items.
- [Interactive documents](interactive-documents.md): quotes, agreements and approvals your clients open from a link,
  sign and download as PDF.
- [Time tracking](time-tracking.md): timers, manual time entries and the time log.
- [Calendar and Google sync](calendar-and-google-sync.md): calendar views, Google Calendar sync, Hebrew dates,
  holidays and working days.
