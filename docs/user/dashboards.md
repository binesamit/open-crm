# Dashboards

Hebrew version: [dashboards.he.md](dashboards.he.md)

## What it's for

A dashboard sums up several boards on one page: numbers, charts, status batteries, a calendar, a timeline, workload, hours and more. Use it to see how work is going across projects, teams or clients without opening each board.

Dashboards are part of the plan. If your plan does not include them, "New dashboard" shows as unavailable. Account members and admins can create dashboards; account viewers and guests cannot.

## Create a dashboard

1. In the sidebar, click "Add new" and choose "New dashboard". To put it in a folder, use "New dashboard here" from the folder's menu.
2. Click the name at the top to rename it.
3. Click "Boards" and tick the boards it should read ("The dashboard's boards"). A dashboard can read up to 50 boards, from any workspace.
4. Click "Add widget" and pick a widget type.

## Widget types

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| "Numbers" | One number: "Count items", or the sum, average, median, minimum or maximum of a numbers column. You can add text "Before the number" and "After the number". |
| "Chart" | Bars, stacked bars, a line, a pie or a donut. "X axis" can be the board, the group or a column; dates are grouped by day, week, month, quarter or year. |
| "Status battery" | How the items split across the labels of a status column. |
| "Items table" | A list of items with the columns you choose. |
| "Calendar" | Items on a month by a date column. |
| "Timeline" | Items as bars on a time axis. |
| "Workload" | How much each person has, by a people column. |
| "Time report" | Hours from time-tracking columns, grouped by user, board, item, day or a column (for example a client), for a period you choose. "Billable hours only" is optional. |
| "Board view" | A saved view of a board, inside the dashboard. |
| "Text" | Free text, such as a title or a note. |
| "Extension widget" | A widget from an installed extension. See [Extensions](extensions.md). |

## Set up a widget

1. Open the widget's menu and choose "Settings".
2. Set the "Title".
3. Under "Boards", tick the boards this widget reads. None ticked means all of the dashboard's boards.
4. Under "Count", choose "Items", "Subitems" or "Both".
5. Choose the columns. Boards don't need the same columns: for each board you pick "The column on {board}" (or "Not on this board").
6. Add a "Widget filter" if needed: "Add condition", and "All conditions" or "Any condition".
7. "Save".

## Arrange the layout

- Drag a widget by its top to move it ("Drag to move").
- Drag its corner to resize it ("Drag to resize").
- To remove a widget, open its menu and choose "Delete".

A dashboard can hold up to 60 widgets.

## See the items behind a number (drill-down)

Click a number, a chart bar, a battery segment or a person. A list of the items it is made of opens ("Show the items: ..."). Click an item to open it right there, without leaving the dashboard. "Show more" loads more, "Close the list" closes it.

To turn this off for a widget, untick "A click shows the items" in its settings.

## Filter the whole dashboard

The bar at the top of the dashboard filters every widget at once:

- "Search all widgets" narrows by text.
- "Person" shows "Everyone" or "Me" (or a specific person).
- Owners and editors can click "Save the filter for everyone" so everyone starts with it.

## Ask AI about a dashboard

If the AI assistant is on in your account, open "AI assistant" on the dashboard. It suggests questions like "What stands out in this data?" and "What is stuck or at risk?". You can also ask it to create a dashboard, or add, change, duplicate, arrange or remove widgets. Every change waits for your approval and can be undone. See [AI](ai.md).

## Share a dashboard

1. Click "Share".
2. Under "Dashboard type" choose:
   - "Main": everyone in the workspace sees it.
   - "Private": only the people invited see it.
   - "Shareable": only the people invited, and guests can be invited too.
3. "Add a person or team", and set their "Access": "Owner" (edits, shares, deletes), "Editor" (edits widgets, layout, boards and the filter) or "Viewer".
4. To invite people who are not in the account yet, use "Invite by email" and choose how "New people join as". Guests can only be invited to a shareable dashboard.

Account admins can always manage every dashboard.

Each person sees in the widgets only what their own board permissions allow. If a dashboard reads boards you cannot see, it shows "{n} hidden boards" and those boards are not counted for you.

## Duplicate, copy a link, favorite or delete

Open the dashboard's menu (the "..." next to its name):

- "Add to favorites" puts it in the sidebar's "Favorites".
- "Copy link" copies its address.
- "Duplicate dashboard" makes a copy (members and admins).
- "Delete" moves it to the trash, with "Undo". Only owners can delete.

## A dashboard inside a board

To see widgets for one board only, add a "Dashboard" view to the board (see [Views](views.md)). Its widgets read items, subitems or both of that board. In the view settings, "The view's filter applies to the widgets" makes the widgets follow the view's filter, search and person.

## Limits

- Up to 50 boards and 60 widgets per dashboard.
- Numbers are calculated on the first 5,000 items of each board. When a board has more, the dashboard says so.
